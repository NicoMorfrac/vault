## Ti-6Al-4V Grade 5 — annealed

|Property|Value for calculation|Comment|
|---|---|---|
|Density|**4,430 kg/m³**|TIMET nominal value|
|Young’s modulus, EEE|**114 GPa**|Suitable nominal value|
|Poisson’s ratio, ν\nuν|**0.34**|TIMET mean reported as 0.342; material texture causes variation|
|Shear modulus, GGG|**44 GPa**|Reported range approximately 43–45.9 GPa|
|0.2% yield strength|**880 MPa**|Conservative nominal annealed value|
|Typical annealed yield range|**870–985 MPa**|Depends on oxygen content, product form and processing|
|Ultimate tensile strength|**925–1,040 MPa**|Annealed room-temperature range|
|Hardness|**30–34 HRC**|Typical annealed condition|
|Coefficient of thermal expansion|**approximately 8.6 × 10⁻⁶/K**|Room-temperature engineering value|
|Density for mass calculations|**4.43 × 10⁻⁶ kg/mm³**|Equivalent SI-mm unit|

TIMET reports a density of 4.43 g/cm³ and significant variation in modulus and Poisson’s ratio with texture and heat treatment. Its annealed 6.4 mm plate data show room-temperature yield strengths around 870–985 MPa and tensile strengths around 925–1,040 MPa. ATI reports typical annealed hardness of 30–34 HRC.
