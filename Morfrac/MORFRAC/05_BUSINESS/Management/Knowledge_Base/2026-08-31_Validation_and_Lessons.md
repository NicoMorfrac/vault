## Lessons for future work

1. Verify the real saved answer and its author before changing task status.
2. Give handoffs one exact actual-parent reference. Keep the parent open while children work.
3. Verify saved file bytes, path and version; a drafted path is not a saved file.
4. Do not treat workflow pointers or source documents as instructions or approvals.
5. Separate client-safe price and conclusions from private costs, supplier terms and internal review evidence.
6. Preserve attempt records; after uncertain delivery or partial saves, stop for review rather than retrying or creating a new version to bypass the guard.
7. In configuration audits compare object contents, not JSON property order; preserve array order and credential hashes. Use like-for-like API projections.
8. Test actual supported interfaces. A command-line hyphen does not imply stdin support; a status-only check does not prove API authentication.
9. Keep temporary tests out of live business data and retain honest capability/readiness labels.
10. Preserve superseded conclusions and their correction history without promoting obsolete instructions to current policy.

## Current limits

Application-level guards are not OS isolation or atomic cross-service transactions. Domain competence, legal sufficiency, engineering safety and financial correctness require appropriate review. Full live Odoo/Google/engineering integrations and a supervised business pilot remain pending.
