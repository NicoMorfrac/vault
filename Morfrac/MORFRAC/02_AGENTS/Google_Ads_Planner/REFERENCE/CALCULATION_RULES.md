# Calculation and Forecast Rules

## Principles

- Use consistent currency and time periods.
- Separate media spend from taxes, fees, tools, creative, landing pages, and labour.
- Reconcile subtotal to total.
- Retain more precision during calculation and round only presentation values.
- Show formulas and source every input.
- Use ranges when uncertainty is material.
- Never use a platform recommendation as proof of business value.

## Common calculations

- CTR = clicks / impressions
- CPC = cost / clicks
- Conversion rate = conversions / clicks
- CPA = cost / conversions
- ROAS = attributed revenue / media spend
- Lead-to-sale rate = sales / qualified leads or leads, but state denominator
- Expected gross profit per lead = gross profit per sale x lead-to-sale rate

Do not calculate when the denominator is zero or definitions are inconsistent.

## Scenario labels

- Historical observation: measured account/export value.
- Platform forecast: dated output from Google or another named planning tool.
- Commercial input: value supplied by a named owner.
- Planning assumption: unverified value used to test sensitivity.

Never mix these labels silently.

## Decision safety

- No scenario may exceed the human-supplied spend cap.
- Do not use average revenue in place of gross profit without stating the limitation.
- Do not claim break-even from ROAS alone when product mix, tax, fulfilment, returns, or sales costs are absent.
- For B2B leads, include lead quality and close-rate uncertainty before discussing return.
