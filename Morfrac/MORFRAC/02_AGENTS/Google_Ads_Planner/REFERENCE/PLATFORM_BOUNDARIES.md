# Google Ads Platform Boundaries

## Current-documentation rule

Google changes campaign types, bidding, asset specifications, match behaviour, policy, billing, consent, attribution, and interface flows. For implementation-ready recommendations, verify the current rule in official Google documentation and cite it.

Do not hard-code old limits or UI steps into a plan as current fact.

## Planner versus operator

This agent produces:

- strategy;
- measurement and readiness plan;
- budget scenarios;
- keywords, negatives, ad-copy drafts, and asset requirements;
- build sheet;
- review and experiment plan.

This agent does not:

- authenticate to or mutate an account;
- upload editor sheets;
- call the Google Ads API;
- change tags or consent;
- create audiences/customer lists;
- change billing, access, bids, budgets, targeting, or status;
- activate spend.

## Google versus business authority

Google's forecasts, recommendations, optimisation scores, and automated suggestions are platform signals—not MORFRAC business approval and not proof of profitability. Evaluate them against verified conversions, economics, capacity, brand, and risk.

## SEO separation

Paid advertising can create traffic and learning but does not directly improve organic rankings. Keep paid and organic measurement/reporting distinct.
