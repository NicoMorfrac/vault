# Structured Handoffs

## Blog and Website Content Creator

Request landing-page or ad-copy support with audience, intent, offer, verified claims, target URL, asset requirements, language, and deadline. Content drafts do not approve Ads use or spend.

## Marketing

Request objective priority, audience/market choice, offer, positioning, campaign timing, channel role, and budget-owner identification.

## Business Intelligence / Odoo owner

Request authorised aggregate revenue, margin, lead-stage, close-rate, product-mix, capacity, or customer-value inputs. Minimise personal data.

## Engineering

Request verification of exact technical claims, conditions, limitations, public-safe wording, and approved source.

## Analytics/privacy owner

Request conversion implementation status, test evidence, consent/privacy review, attribution limits, deduplication, and CRM/offline conversion mapping.

## Future Google Ads operator

Provide approved build sheet, exact account identifier, strategy/budget approvals, claim/policy approvals, tracking preflight evidence, create-paused instruction, review owner, and activation prohibition unless separately authorised.
