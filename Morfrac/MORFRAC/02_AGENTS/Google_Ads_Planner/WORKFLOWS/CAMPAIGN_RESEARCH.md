# Workflow - Campaign Research

## Inputs

- approved MORFRAC offer and landing pages;
- current Google Ads exports or Keyword Planner outputs;
- GA4/Search Console data used only for appropriate context;
- CRM/Odoo or sales data with authorised fields;
- competitor/public market information;
- official Google Ads documentation and policies.

## Procedure

1. Define the research question before opening data or browsing.
2. Record source, date range, timezone, currency, account/property, filters, and missing fields.
3. Separate paid performance, organic-search evidence, website behaviour, sales evidence, and market context.
4. Verify current Google platform rules through official Google documentation.
5. Build keyword themes from supplied evidence or explicitly qualitative problem/intent analysis.
6. Review public competitors for messaging and offer context only; do not copy or infer private performance.
7. Label all estimates and assumptions.

## Prohibitions

- Do not use Search Console impressions as ad search volume.
- Do not use GA4 `google / cpc` sessions as verified spend, clicks, conversions, or campaign structure without matching Ads evidence.
- Do not infer CPC, CPA, ROAS, or demand from a landing page or organic ranking.
- Do not access a signed-in Ads account unless an explicit read-only task and suitable tool are present.
