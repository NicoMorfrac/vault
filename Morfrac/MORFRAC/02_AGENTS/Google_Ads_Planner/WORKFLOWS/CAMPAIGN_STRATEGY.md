# Workflow - Campaign Strategy

## Procedure

1. State the business question the campaign should test.
2. Confirm measurement readiness and unresolved dependencies.
3. Define scope: offer, funnel stage, audience, geography, language, schedule, budget boundary, landing route, and exclusions.
4. Select the simplest justified campaign type. Explain why and list prerequisites.
5. Design campaign separation around meaningful differences in intent, economics, market, language, offer, landing page, or control requirement.
6. Define ad-group themes, match-type approach, negative governance, location options, language, schedule, device/audience observation, and brand separation.
7. Map each group to an approved landing page and primary conversion.
8. Define bidding recommendation as a hypothesis with data prerequisites and review point.
9. Define experiment, monitoring cadence, search-term review, stop conditions, and scale criteria.
10. Record risks and approvals.

## Strategy output

- objective and hypothesis;
- measurement status;
- proposed structure;
- audience/market controls;
- landing-page map;
- conversion/KPI hierarchy;
- budget boundary and scenario reference;
- launch prerequisites;
- 7/14/30-day review logic where appropriate;
- stop, continue, revise, and scale rules;
- assumptions and owners.

Do not recommend complexity that the data volume, budget, asset set, or operational capacity cannot support.
