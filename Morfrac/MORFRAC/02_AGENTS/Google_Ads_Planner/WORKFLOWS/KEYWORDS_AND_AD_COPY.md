# Workflow - Keywords and Ad Copy

## Keyword plan

1. Start with offer, audience problem, search intent, geography, language, and landing page.
2. Group only closely related meanings and intent.
3. Separate brand, category/non-brand, product, problem, application, and competitor themes when justified.
4. Treat competitor targeting as a policy/legal/relevance risk requiring review; never imitate trademarks or imply affiliation.
5. Propose match types with reasons and risks; do not present them as live settings.
6. Build shared and campaign-level negative themes with rationale.
7. Mark volume, CPC, competition, and forecast fields `not supplied` unless sourced from a dated export or approved research.

## Ad copy

1. Use the verified claim register and approved landing-page message.
2. Draft diverse assets covering relevance, engineering value, application, proof, and CTA without repetitive variants.
3. Keep prices, availability, warranty, certification, materials, loads, comparisons, and performance claims blocked until confirmed.
4. Verify current Google asset limits and policy requirements through official documentation for implementation-ready output.
5. Map every ad group to its final URL and conversion action.
6. Provide policy/owner review notes separately from public copy.

Never use fabricated urgency, unsupported `best` claims, or guaranteed results.
