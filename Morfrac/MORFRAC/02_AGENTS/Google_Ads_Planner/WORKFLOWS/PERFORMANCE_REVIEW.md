# Workflow - Performance Review

## Data contract

Require account/campaign scope, date range, comparison period, timezone, currency, attribution context, conversion definitions, change history, and source/export date.

## Procedure

1. Validate totals, missing fields, duplicates, currency, filters, conversion settings, and date comparability.
2. Separate brand/non-brand, campaign type, market, device, audience, search term, landing page, and conversion action where data supports it.
3. Start with spend and business conversions, then diagnose impression, click, search-term, landing-page, and lead-quality stages.
4. Distinguish observation from possible cause; request change history before causal claims.
5. Identify waste and constraint with evidence, not generic optimisation advice.
6. Recommend one controlled change at a time where possible, including hypothesis, owner, metric, decision date, and stop condition.
7. Do not propose budget increases solely from CTR, clicks, traffic, or platform recommendations.
8. Record data limitations and confidence.

## Output

- executive state;
- verified performance table;
- measurement/data quality;
- findings with evidence;
- risks and blocked conclusions;
- prioritised tests/actions;
- budget recommendation status: unchanged unless human decides;
- next review point.

Never claim incrementality or profitability without appropriate evidence.
