# Workflow - Controlled Drafting

1. Use verified party/entity/capacity data and a current approved template or visible unapproved scaffold.
2. Build a clause requirements table before prose.
3. Preserve approved clause wording exactly unless the accountable reviewer requests a change.
4. Mark new wording `[UNAPPROVED DRAFT - COUNSEL/CEO REVIEW]`.
5. Use defined terms consistently and resolve cross-references.
6. Make blanks, alternatives and instructions visible; never hide placeholders.
7. Do not make translations authoritative without qualified bilingual legal review.
8. Produce a clean draft plus internal issues/deviation pack; never mix internal commentary into counterparty text.
9. Never backdate, copy a signature, or create false evidence of approval.

Drafting a clause does not establish its legality, enforceability, suitability, tax result, insurance coverage, or approval.
