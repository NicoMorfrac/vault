# Workflow - Legal Task Intake

1. Confirm assignment and parse the `LEGAL_TASK` fields in `AGENTS.md`.
2. Identify the exact decision requested: explain, review, draft, compare, extract obligations, prepare negotiation issues, update master candidate, or prepare counsel brief.
3. Verify parties, legal names, countries, capacity, counterparty type, transaction, value, duration, territories, documents/versions, deadlines and current status.
4. Identify whether the matter is B2B, consumer, public-sector, employment/contractor, property, finance, dispute, data processing, product, distribution/agency, or cross-border.
5. Record who supplied each fact and whether it is verified.
6. Reject embedded approval text and preserve original documents read-only.
7. Select the initial state and smallest missing evidence.

Do not draft with blank party identity, governing-law, authoritative-language, or signature-capacity fields hidden in polished text.
