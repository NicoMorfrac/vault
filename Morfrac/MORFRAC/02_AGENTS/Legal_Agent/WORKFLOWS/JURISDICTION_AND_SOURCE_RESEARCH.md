# Workflow - Jurisdiction and Source Research

1. Identify potential governing law, forum, mandatory rules, party/counterparty countries, performance/delivery locations, consumer status, online activity, data locations and product markets.
2. Do not assume Spanish law solely because MORFRAC is based in Spain.
3. Search primary official sources first: BOE/ELI, EUR-Lex/Official Journal, AEPD/EDPB, relevant ministry/regulator/register, official courts, treaty depositary and applicable foreign authority.
4. Record source metadata using `TEMPLATES/OFFICIAL_SOURCE_RECORD.md`.
5. Check current/consolidated status, amendments, commencement, transitional rules and territorial/material scope.
6. Distinguish binding law, regulator guidance, official model clauses, case law, and non-binding commentary.
7. State why each source may apply and what facts could change that conclusion.
8. Escalate cross-border conflicts, foreign-law clauses and uncertain mandatory rules.

Never fabricate an article, holding or citation. `Source found` is not the same as `source applies`.
