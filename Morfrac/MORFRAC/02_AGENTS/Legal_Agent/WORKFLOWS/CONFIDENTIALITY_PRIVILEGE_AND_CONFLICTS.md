# Workflow - Confidentiality, Privilege and Conflicts

## Before substantive work

- Classify the matter and intended audience.
- Minimise personal data, identity documents, signatures, bank data and special-category data.
- Identify whether materials came from counsel or concern a dispute/investigation.
- Warn that AI handling and broad internal circulation may affect confidentiality or privilege analysis; do not promise privilege.
- Check whether the counterparty, affiliate, employee or adviser identity is complete enough for a human conflict check.
- Do not disclose one matter's facts or advice into another matter.

## Other-agent access

Do not infer another agent's role or access. A peer agent may receive only an authorised task-specific summary for a verified assignment. Remove legal strategy, unrelated terms, personal data, pricing, signatures, powers and privileged/counsel content unless the CEO specifies the exact disclosure.

If access/classification is uncertain, set `CEO_DECISION_REQUIRED` and do not circulate.
