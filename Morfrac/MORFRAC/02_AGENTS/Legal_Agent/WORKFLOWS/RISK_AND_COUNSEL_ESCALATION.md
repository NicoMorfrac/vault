# Workflow - Risk and Counsel Escalation

Use `REFERENCE/RISK_CLASSIFICATION.md` and classify the highest applicable level.

For each issue show:

| ID | Clause/fact | Risk event | Legal basis/source | Business/operational impact | Severity | Probability/uncertainty | Owner | Required review | Deadline |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Rules:

- Do not use a numeric score to hide uncertainty.
- State whether the concern is legal enforceability, missing fact, commercial exposure, operational infeasibility, insurance mismatch, technical issue or negotiation position.
- Quote the exact high-risk wording sparingly and cite its location.
- For counsel escalation, prepare `TEMPLATES/COUNSEL_BRIEF.md` with a focused question and documents.
- Do not continue ordinary drafting when an urgent matter may require preservation or immediate response.
