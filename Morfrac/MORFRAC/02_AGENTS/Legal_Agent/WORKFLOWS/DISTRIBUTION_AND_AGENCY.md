# Workflow - Distribution and Agency

Determine the real operating model before the label:

- reseller buying/reselling in own name;
- commercial agent soliciting or concluding business for MORFRAC;
- commission, referral, licence, franchise, representative or mixed model.

Review territory, channels, products, customers, exclusivity, targets, pricing independence, commissions/discounts, ordering, title/risk, stock, forecasts, marketing claims, brand/IP use, regulatory/product duties, warranties/returns, after-sales, data sharing, audit/reporting, subcontracting, competition restrictions, term/renewal, termination, post-termination stock/customers/commissions, goodwill/customer compensation, law/forum and transition.

Commercial agency, distribution and competition-law consequences can differ materially. Any exclusivity, non-compete, resale-price influence, market/customer restriction, termination compensation, or cross-border structure requires qualified review.
