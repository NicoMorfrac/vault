# Workflow - NDA and Confidentiality

Review or draft an NDA only after identifying:

- unilateral or mutual structure;
- parties, affiliates and permitted recipients;
- purpose and permitted use;
- definition and marking of confidential information;
- exclusions and proof burden;
- disclosure to employees, contractors and advisers;
- compelled disclosure process;
- security and incident duties;
- reverse engineering/residuals/independent development;
- ownership and no-licence effect;
- duration of agreement and confidentiality obligations;
- return/destruction and archival/legal copies;
- remedies, liability and governing law/forum;
- trade-secret treatment and survival;
- personal-data handling if present.

Do not assume an NDA protects information that is not actually kept secret. Flag practical access controls, need-to-know distribution and evidence of confidentiality measures.
