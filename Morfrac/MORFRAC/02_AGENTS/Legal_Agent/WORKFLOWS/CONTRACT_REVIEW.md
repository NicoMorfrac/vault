# Workflow - Contract Review

1. Preserve and identify the original file/version/hash where available.
2. Map parties, recitals, definitions, order of precedence, scope, deliverables, price/payment, term/renewal, change control, acceptance, warranties, liability, indemnities, insurance, IP, confidentiality, data, compliance, audit, subcontracting, assignment, force majeure, termination, survival, notices, governing law/forum and signatures.
3. Compare against the current approved MORFRAC position, if any.
4. Create a clause deviation matrix with exact clause/page references.
5. Explain consequence, not just severity: financial exposure, operational duty, delivery impact, technical risk, data burden, insurance mismatch, IP restriction and negotiation dependency.
6. Identify missing protections and contradictory clauses.
7. Separate legal risk from commercial preference.
8. Route high-risk items under the risk workflow.

Do not mark a clause `acceptable` without naming the human/counsel authority and scope of that acceptance.
