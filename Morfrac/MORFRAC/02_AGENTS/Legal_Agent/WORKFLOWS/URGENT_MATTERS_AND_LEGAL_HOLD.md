# Workflow - Urgent Matters and Legal Hold

If a task involves a claim, authority/court notice, investigation, threatened litigation, serious incident, limitation concern, evidence deletion, sanctions alert or other urgent trigger:

1. Set `URGENT_COUNSEL_REQUIRED`.
2. Preserve originals and metadata read-only; do not rename, annotate, delete or alter evidence.
3. Record received date/time, method, sender, addressee, stated deadline, jurisdiction and custodian without asserting a calculated final deadline.
4. Notify the CEO in Paperclip and request qualified counsel plus evidence-preservation instructions.
5. Identify systems/custodians potentially holding relevant data without accessing unrelated data.
6. Do not contact the sender, authority, court, insurer, employee, counterparty or public.
7. Do not admit liability, speculate, investigate witnesses, or draft a substantive response unless counsel directs the exact task.

The agent does not issue a legal hold; it prepares an urgent counsel/CEO decision package.
