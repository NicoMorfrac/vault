# Workflow - Legal Master Library

Candidate controlled records include:

- verified company legal identity and registered details;
- signatory/power register references;
- counsel contacts and scope;
- approved templates and clause positions;
- playbooks and negotiation limits;
- official-source register;
- contract/matter register schema;
- legal retention schedule approved by counsel/owner.

Never promote a candidate from one matter automatically.

Before master approval, post exact record/path, current and proposed version, source, author, reviewing counsel/CEO, risk, effective date, expiry/review date, superseded record and affected templates. Require exact `APPROVE LEGAL MASTER <Issue-ID>`.

Append a revision and preserve history. Do not overwrite an approved or executed record. The vault is not automatically the authoritative corporate register; record the designated source of truth.
