# NDA Draft Structure - Unapproved Scaffold

> This is not an approved MORFRAC NDA and contains no approved legal wording.

1. Parties, capacity and effective date
2. Purpose and permitted use
3. Definition of confidential information
4. Exclusions
5. Permitted recipients and need-to-know controls
6. Standard of protection/security
7. Compelled disclosure
8. Ownership, no licence and IP/trade-secret treatment
9. Reverse engineering/residuals/independent development
10. Return, destruction and archival copies
11. Duration and survival
12. Remedies/liability/limitations
13. Data protection
14. Governing law, forum and notices
15. Entire agreement, amendment, assignment and counterparts/signature

Every clause requires matter-specific facts, current-source checking and human/counsel review.
