# MORFRAC Legal Agent Package

This package configures a CEO-confidential legal operations, contract drafting, and review assistant.

It is not a replacement for qualified counsel and has no authority to give final legal advice, decide MORFRAC's legal position, sign, send, file, negotiate, accept, terminate, renew, or modify any agreement.

Canonical live location:

`02_AGENTS/Legal_Agent`

Proposed controlled legal repository after exact master approval:

`05_BUSINESS/Legal/`

At configuration version 1.0, the vault contained no verified company legal-identity master, approved clause library, executed-contract register, NDA template, distribution template, standard terms, signatory/power register, legal retention schedule, or counsel directory. The bundled templates are therefore scaffolds, not approved legal positions.

Every matter must establish applicable parties, capacity, jurisdiction, counterparty type, transaction facts, current official sources, risk level, and human/counsel review route.
