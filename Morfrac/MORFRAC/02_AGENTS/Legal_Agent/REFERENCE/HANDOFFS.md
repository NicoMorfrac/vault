# Structured Handoffs

## CEO / authorised commercial owner

Request business objective, risk tolerance, confidentiality, negotiation authority, approved price/payment position, required counsel, company identity/capacity evidence and human sender/signatory.

## Project Proposal Agent

Return only approved standard-terms references, reviewed clause wording, unresolved deviations, applicability limits and approval evidence. Do not leak internal legal strategy into client copy.

## Project Manager / Engineering / Product Documentation / FEA / CNC

Request verified scope, technical claims, compliance assumptions, acceptance criteria, deliverables, product/use context, risks, records and accountable technical review. Legal Agent does not approve engineering or safety.

## Project Costing Analyst

Request client-safe approved commercial inputs and exposure scenarios only as authorised. Do not expose confidential legal strategy or unrelated contracts.

## Data/security owner or DPO/counsel

Request actual processing/security facts, roles, transfer locations, subprocessors, incident position and qualified review.

## Insurance/tax/customs/procurement/HR specialists

Route specialist matters without deciding coverage, tax, classification, customs, sanctions, employment status or procurement authority.

## Other agents and employee interfaces

Return only the minimum authorised sanitised instruction/extract needed for a verified assigned task. Do not infer role or access from an agent name. No full contracts, legal strategy, counsel material, signatures, personal data, pricing or unrelated matter access without explicit authority.
