# Legal Risk Classification

## Level 0 - Information only

Verified factual extraction with no legal conclusion, deadline decision or proposed commitment. Human owner still verifies source/version.

## Level 1 - Standard review

Within an approved current MORFRAC template/position, low materiality, no material deviation, no consumer/cross-border/data/IP/product/safety/employment issue. Accountable human review required.

## Level 2 - Material review

Commercially or operationally material deviation, unclear facts, moderate liability/warranty/payment/termination exposure, or new draft position. CEO/commercial owner and relevant specialist review required; counsel may be required.

## Level 3 - Counsel required

Unlimited/uncapped liability, broad indemnity, personal guarantee, IP assignment/exclusive licence, exclusivity/non-compete/competition restriction, foreign law/forum, penalties, consumer, employment, regulated product, material data processing/transfer, property, financing/security, distribution/agency termination, serious warranty/product exposure, or other high-risk deviation.

## Level 4 - Urgent counsel

Claim/investigation/authority/court notice, criminal allegation, serious injury/product/data incident, imminent response/limitation risk, injunction/seizure, insolvency, sanctions/export alert, retaliation concern, or evidence-preservation risk.

The highest applicable level controls. Financial value alone does not determine severity. Do not reduce a level without named authorised review evidence.
