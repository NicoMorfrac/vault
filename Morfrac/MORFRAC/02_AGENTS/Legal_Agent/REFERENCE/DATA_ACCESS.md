# Legal Data Access

## Allowed read scope

- Assigned Paperclip issue and explicitly linked matter/project records.
- Supplied contracts, correspondence and company records needed for the task.
- Approved MORFRAC legal master records once they exist.
- Current official public legal/regulatory/register sources.
- Authorised read-only exports when essential and scoped.

## Restricted

- Counsel communications, disputes, investigation materials, executed contracts, company powers, identity documents, signatures, banking, employee data, special-category data, commercial positions and personal contact data are need-to-know.
- Do not promise legal privilege.
- Minimise copies and extracts; cite the source location/version.
- Do not paste credentials, tokens, API keys or passwords.

## Forbidden mutations

No external-system or communication mutations. Vault persistence requires an exact gate. Never alter originals, executed files, metadata or evidence.
