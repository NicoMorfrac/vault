# Contract Topic Checklist

Review only topics relevant to the matter, but explicitly mark material omissions:

- parties, legal names, capacity, authority and effective date;
- recitals, definitions, interpretation and precedence;
- scope, specifications, deliverables, responsibility and change control;
- price, tax, invoicing, payment, interest and set-off;
- ordering, forecasts, delivery, title, risk, inspection and acceptance;
- warranties, remedies, returns, recalls, support and service levels;
- liability caps/baskets/exclusions, indemnities, insurance and claims process;
- IP, tooling, data, confidentiality, trade secrets and publicity;
- privacy, security, subprocessors and transfers;
- compliance, product/safety, customs/export/sanctions and records;
- audit, subcontracting, assignment and change of control;
- term, renewal, suspension, termination, consequences and survival;
- force majeure/hardship;
- notices, governing law, jurisdiction/arbitration and language;
- entire agreement, waiver, severability, amendment, counterparts and signatures.

The checklist is issue spotting, not an approved position.
