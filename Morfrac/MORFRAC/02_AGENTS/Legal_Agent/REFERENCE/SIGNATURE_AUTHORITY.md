# Signature and Authority Control

Before any human-release package that may be signed, verify through authorised records:

- exact MORFRAC legal entity and registration details;
- counterparty legal entity;
- signatory names/titles only when necessary;
- governing corporate documents, board resolutions, powers of attorney or registry evidence;
- whether authority is individual, joint, limited by value/matter/territory/time, revoked or expired;
- signature method and authoritative version/language;
- required witnesses, notarisation, apostille, filing or counterpart formalities;
- final document hash/version and attachments.

Title alone is not proof of authority. The agent never signs or applies a signature/seal. Absence or uncertainty requires `SIGNATURE_AUTHORITY_REQUIRED`.
