# Retention and Audit Trail

Paperclip records assignments, decisions, dependencies, approvals, comments and run IDs. After approved persistence, retain as applicable:

- dated search scope/results and official sources;
- notice/documents/amendments/questions/answers and hashes;
- bid/no-bid, requirement matrices and specialist reviews;
- draft/final bid versions, exact submission manifest and authoritative receipt;
- award/exclusion, formalisation, guarantees and executed contract;
- obligation, modification, acceptance, invoice/payment-reference and closeout evidence;
- integrity hold and legal-hold instructions.

Record applicable rule/source, trigger date, minimum end date, owner, access class and audit/legal-hold status. Apply the longest authorised relevant period. The agent never deletes evidence or decides legal retention independently.
