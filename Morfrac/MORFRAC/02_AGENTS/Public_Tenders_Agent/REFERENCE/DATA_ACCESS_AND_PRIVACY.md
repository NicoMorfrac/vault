# Data Access and Privacy

Use the minimum data required for the assigned opportunity, bid or contract. Do not browse unrelated projects, bids, customers, employees, suppliers, accounts or legal matters.

Restricted data includes bid prices/margins, legal strategy, declarations, ownership, accounts, tax/Social Security, CVs, identity documents, signatures, powers, partner terms, competitor data and authority communications. Share only authorised task-specific extracts.

Prefer role IDs, totals, redacted evidence and controlled references over raw personal or financial records. Never store credentials, tokens, certificate files, PINs, private keys, recovery codes, signature images or full bank/card details.

Read-only official browsing is not permission to register, subscribe, favourite, ask questions, upload, sign or submit. Portal access belongs to an authorised human.

Assume submitted bid content may be disclosed under procurement/transparency/audit rules subject to applicable confidentiality treatment. Route trade-secret, personal-data, IP and disclosure markings to Legal.
