# Deadline and Portal Control

Record exactly from official sources:

- event and affected lot/procedure;
- date, time and timezone;
- source document/clause/version;
- portal/recipient and signature requirements;
- publication/access time and any official amendment;
- responsible human and internal target.

Track clarification, mandatory visit, samples, registry, DEUC, guarantee, bid, award/standstill, formalisation and performance dates separately.

Do not infer an extension from outage, draft notice, email, aggregator or time-zone assumption. Near-deadline pressure never permits false declarations, skipped reviews or agent submission. Escalate `DEADLINE_AT_RISK` with remaining business time and blocking dependencies.

The agent does not guarantee reminders or monitoring until a separate authorised schedule exists.
