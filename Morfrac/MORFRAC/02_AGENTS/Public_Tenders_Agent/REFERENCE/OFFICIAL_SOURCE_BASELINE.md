# Official Source Baseline

Verified 2026-08-30. Recheck current availability, amendments and tender-specific applicability at every use.

## Spain

- Plataforma de Contratación del Sector Público (PLACSP), searches and official profiles: https://contrataciondelestado.es/wps/portal/plataforma
- PLACSP open data, including hosted and aggregated notices: https://contrataciondelestado.es/wps/portal/DatosAbiertos
- Ley 9/2017 de Contratos del Sector Público, current consolidated access plus official publication: https://www.boe.es/eli/es/l/2017/11/08/9/con
- Registro Oficial de Licitadores y Empresas Clasificadas del Sector Público (ROLECSP/ROLECE): https://registrodelicitadores.gob.es/rolece/public/inicio.action
- General government public-procurement access guidance: https://administracion.gob.es/pag_Home/Tramites/miEmpresaEnTramites/ContratacionPublica.html

## Catalonia

- Plataforma de Serveis de Contractació Pública (PSCP): https://contractaciopublica.cat/es/inici
- PSCP operator/company area and DEUC service: https://contractaciopublica.cat/ca/empreses-licitadores
- Registre d'empreses licitadores i classificades de Catalunya (RELIC): https://tramits.gencat.cat/ca/tramits/tramits-temes/8428-registre-electronic-empreses-licitadores-relic

## European Union

- Tenders Electronic Daily (TED), Supplement to the Official Journal: https://ted.europa.eu/en
- TED description/search capabilities: https://ted.europa.eu/en/about-ted
- Directive 2014/24/EU on public procurement: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2014/24/oj
- Directive 2014/25/EU for utilities sectors: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2014/25/oj
- European Single Procurement Document and e-Certis: https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/single-market/public-procurement/digital-procurement/european-single-procurement-document-and-ecertis_en
- Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2016/7 ESPD form: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2016/7/oj

## Use limits

The tender's official profile, notice, current clauses/specifications, annexes, amendments and official answers control the specific bid. Consolidated legislation and guidance support review but do not replace the official publication or qualified legal advice. Portal/registry content can change and must be dated. Licensed standards may be cited but not reproduced without rights.
