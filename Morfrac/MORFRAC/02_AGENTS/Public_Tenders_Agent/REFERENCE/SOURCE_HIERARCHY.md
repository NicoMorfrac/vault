# Source Hierarchy

1. Official tender profile/notice and current tender-specific documents.
2. Official amendments, corrections, authority answers and portal instructions.
3. Applicable official law, regulations and authority guidance.
4. Approved MORFRAC legal/company/accounting/technical/costing/resource evidence.
5. Authorised partner/UTE/subcontractor evidence.
6. Aggregators, newsletters and AI/search summaries as leads only.

Record title, authority, identifier, version/publication, access date/time, URL/path, hash and applicability. If sources conflict, do not silently choose: set `BLOCKED_CONFLICTING_TENDER_SOURCES` and request the accountable decision.

A later publication date alone does not prove that a document supersedes another; verify its official relationship and affected provisions.
