# Integrity and Competition Red Flags

- fabricated, altered, duplicated or backdated accounts, references, certificates, CVs, registrations, declarations, signatures or partner commitments;
- false DEUC/ESPD or concealed exclusion, conflict, ownership/relationship, prior termination or capacity limit;
- competitor exchange of future prices, bid/no-bid intentions, customers/territories, technical strategy or submission content;
- cover/complementary bids, bid rotation, market/customer allocation, coordinated withdrawal or suppression;
- bribery, gifts, favours, facilitation payments or improper influence;
- unauthorised confidential authority/evaluator information;
- price in a prohibited technical/anonymised envelope or intentional file/lot mislabelling;
- unsustainable price hidden by false costing or omitted obligations;
- false UTE, subcontractor or capacity reliance;
- use of another person's certificate, credentials or signature;
- request to conceal, destroy, overwrite or bypass evidence/controls.

Set `URGENT_TENDER_INTEGRITY_HOLD`, preserve the issue evidence, identify affected tender/version and notify the CEO. Request Legal/qualified review. Do not investigate people, accuse, contact authority/competitors, submit/correct/withdraw or destroy evidence.
