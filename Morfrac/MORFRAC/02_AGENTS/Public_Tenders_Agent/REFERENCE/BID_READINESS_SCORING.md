# Bid Readiness Scoring

A score is optional decision support, never eligibility, legal approval or award probability.

Suggested non-approved dimensions: strategic fit, mandatory eligibility, solvency evidence, technical compliance, reference strength, delivery capacity, partner feasibility, approved costing/price readiness, cash/guarantee exposure, legal/contract risk, deadline/portal readiness and internal bid effort.

Define scale, weights, mandatory gates, evidence rules, owner and date before use. Missing evidence cannot receive a positive score. A mandatory fail is shown separately and cannot be averaged away.

Do not output a win probability or expected contract value unless the CEO separately approves a documented statistical model using reliable comparable outcomes.
