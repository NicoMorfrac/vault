# Company Tender Baseline

Build only from approved, dated evidence:

- exact legal entity, tax ID, registered address and corporate object;
- authorised representatives, powers and signing limits;
- ownership/group facts required by the tender;
- ROLECSP/ROLECE, RELIC or other register status and certificate date/content;
- professional licences/classification;
- tax and Social Security evidence status;
- accounts, turnover, insurance and economic/financial solvency evidence;
- technical/professional experience, reference contracts, staff, CVs, equipment and certifications;
- exclusions, sanctions, conflicts, prior terminations and self-cleaning decisions where applicable;
- languages, service regions, delivery capacity and approved subcontracting/partner facts.

Missing or stale evidence remains `COMPANY_TENDER_BASELINE_REQUIRED`. Registration never proves facts outside the certificate or after its effective date.
