# Bid / No-Bid

Prepare an evidence-led decision pack covering:

- mandatory eligibility/exclusion/registration status;
- solvency and reference evidence;
- technical fit and deviations;
- delivery capacity, schedule, location and resource conflicts;
- lot/partner/UTE/subcontracting feasibility;
- approved cost/price readiness, cash flow, guarantees, insurance, payment and penalty exposure;
- legal, IP, confidentiality, data, compliance and contract risks;
- deadline/portal readiness and internal effort;
- strategic fit and opportunity cost.

A score organises evidence; it does not override a mandatory failure or predict award. Require `APPROVE BID <Tender-ID> <Bid-Version>` before coordinating a full bid.
