# Contract Execution Obligations

After human evidence of formalisation, create an obligation register from the executed contract and incorporated tender documents. Track deliverables, milestones, acceptance, reporting, invoicing evidence, quality/service levels, subcontracting, confidentiality, IP/data, insurance/guarantees, penalties, modifications, extensions and notices.

Assign internal owners through Paperclip. Do not interpret an unexecuted draft as binding, certify performance, issue invoices, contact the authority or change Odoo/accounting.

Missing acceptance/payment/evidence remains `EXECUTION_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED`.
