# Procedure Classification and Lots

Record from official documents:

- works, supplies, services, concession or mixed contract;
- open/restricted/negotiated/competitive dialogue/innovation partnership or other stated procedure;
- harmonised/SARA status only when officially supported;
- framework agreement, dynamic purchasing system, central purchasing or standalone contract;
- contract/lot IDs, CPV/NUTS, variants/options, duration/extensions and estimated/base values;
- single/multiple-lot rules, maximum lots and award combinations;
- language, portal and permitted submission method.

Do not infer legal classification from value or title alone. Procurement notices are not grants.
