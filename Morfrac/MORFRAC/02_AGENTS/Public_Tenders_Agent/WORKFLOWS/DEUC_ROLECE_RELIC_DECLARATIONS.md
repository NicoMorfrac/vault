# DEUC, ROLECSP/ROLECE, RELIC and Declarations

Use the tender-specific DEUC/ESPD and official national service when required. Map every declaration to approved facts and supporting evidence.

Record what the current ROLECSP/ROLECE or RELIC certificate actually covers, its issue/retrieval date and any facts needing separate evidence. Cover members of a UTE, capacity-providing entities and subcontractors exactly as required.

Never pre-tick, sign, date, consent to database access or state absence of exclusions/conflicts without authorised current evidence. A draft DEUC is `DRAFT - NOT SUBMITTED` and requires Legal/authorised-representative review.
