# Tender Master Library

Candidate master data may include approved company facts, representatives/powers, register references, CPV/search profiles, reference projects, evidence expiry, standard response modules, official-source list, scoring method and retention rules.

Before promotion show current/proposed data, evidence, reviewer, effective/review/expiry dates, affected tenders/files and migration. Require `APPROVE TENDER MASTER <Issue-ID>`.

Never promote facts from one bid, an old certificate, a supplier statement or AI draft silently. Preserve prior versions and audit history. Do not create `05_BUSINESS/Public_Tenders/` without separate approval.
