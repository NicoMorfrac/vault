# Award, Standstill and Formalisation

Capture the official award/exclusion decision, reasons provided, publication/notification evidence and exact review/standstill/appeal dates without calculating legal deadlines beyond qualified review.

For an award, compare price, scope, conditions and obligations to the submitted bid. Identify guarantees, insurance, certificates, staffing, contract documents, signature and start requirements. Route challenge/appeal, deviations and high-risk terms to Legal.

Require `APPROVE TENDER AWARD <Tender-ID> <Award-Version>` before preparing a human acceptance/formalisation pack. Never accept, waive rights, lodge security or sign.
