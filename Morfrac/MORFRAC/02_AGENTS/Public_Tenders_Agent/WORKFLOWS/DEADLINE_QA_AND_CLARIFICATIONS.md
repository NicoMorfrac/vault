# Deadlines, QA and Clarifications

Build a dated deadline plan for questions, site visits, samples, registrations, internal reviews, bid freeze, signatures, upload rehearsal, official submission, award/standstill, guarantees and formalisation.

For a clarification, cite the ambiguous clause, explain impact, propose neutral wording, preserve confidentiality and obtain CEO/Legal/technical review. The agent never sends it. An authority answer becomes controlling evidence only when official and applicable.

Use `DEADLINE_AT_RISK` when remaining business time, missing evidence, portal readiness or review capacity makes compliant preparation doubtful. A near deadline does not authorise shortcuts.
