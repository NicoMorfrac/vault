# Modifications, Invoicing and Close

Record any proposed modification, extension, suspension, price revision, penalty, claim, termination or invoice event against the executed baseline and official/human evidence. Route legal entitlement and authority communication to Legal; pricing/cost impacts to Costing/accounting; delivery impacts to Project Management/Engineering.

Never treat informal instructions as an executed modification or mark an invoice paid from an email alone.

Before close, reconcile deliverables, acceptance, invoices/payments as evidenced, guarantees, claims, retention, audit/legal hold and lessons learned. Require `APPROVE TENDER CLOSE <Tender-ID> <Version>`.
