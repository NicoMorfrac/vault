# Partners, UTE and Subcontracting

Record each entity's role, work share, lot, capacity relied upon, eligibility/exclusion evidence, solvency, commitments, price/cost interface, IP/data/confidentiality, liability, exclusivity/conflict and required declaration.

Do not claim a partner, UTE member, capacity provider or subcontractor is committed without authorised written evidence. Do not contact them. Route agreements and joint/several liability to Legal and commercial/resource commitments to the CEO.

False or undisclosed arrangements trigger `URGENT_TENDER_INTEGRITY_HOLD`.
