# Exclusion, Capacity, Solvency and Classification

Map each requirement to exact clause, test, entity/lot, permitted evidence, evidence date, owner and status.

Cover legal/personality/representation, professional habilitation, contracting prohibitions, tax/Social Security, economic/financial solvency, technical/professional solvency, classification, standards/certifications and reliance on other entities.

Do not equate a register entry with all current tender requirements. Do not reuse a reference, turnover figure, CV or certificate beyond its evidenced scope/period. Legal/accounting/technical owners decide contested applicability and sufficiency.
