# Commercial Offer and Costing

Translate the official price model into an internal calculation request and client-facing schedule without changing units, quantities, VAT treatment, rounding, options, extensions or lot structure.

Use only approved Costing inputs and CEO/commercial decisions. Separate internal cost/margin/contingency from submitted price. Check arithmetic, totals, formulas, currency, tax assumptions, payment timing, indexation, guarantees, penalties and cash exposure.

Flag abnormal/unsustainable pricing risk; do not optimise to a guessed threshold or approve a price. No price enters the bid until the authorised human decision is evidenced.
