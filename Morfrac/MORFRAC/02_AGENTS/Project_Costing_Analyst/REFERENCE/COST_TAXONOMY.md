# Project Cost Taxonomy

## Labour

- requirements/intake;
- project management and coordination;
- engineering analysis/design/reviews;
- CAD/drawings/documentation;
- procurement/supplier coordination;
- assembly/inspection/test/installation/training;
- closeout/manuals/support.

Use hours x approved fully burdened cost rate when that is MORFRAC's method. Do not use customer bill rate as cost.

## Purchased/external

- materials and components;
- external machining/fabrication/finishing;
- consultants and laboratories;
- prototypes, tooling, jigs, fixtures, NRE;
- certification/inspection/testing;
- software/licensing specifically attributable to the project;
- packing, freight, insurance, customs/duty;
- travel, accommodation, site/installation.

## Indirect/commercial/risk

- approved overhead/indirect allocation;
- warranty/after-sales allowance when policy exists;
- commission/financing/credit cost when applicable;
- escalation and exchange exposure;
- contingency for defined uncertainty;
- management reserve only under approved policy.

## Separate views

- Cost: economic resource consumed by the project under approved treatment.
- Cash: deposits, milestone payments, tax/duty cash, and timing exposure.
- Price: amount proposed to the client after commercial policy.
- Revenue/profit: commercial/accounting outcome, not inferred from cost alone.

Do not double-count a burden inside both a rate and an overhead line.
