## Definitions

- Confirmed: approved current MORFRAC or contractual input.
- Quote: supplier offer with scope and validity.
- Historical: reconciled actual from an identified prior period/project.
- Specialist: estimate supplied by the responsible domain owner.
- Benchmark: public/external budgetary context, not a commitment.
- Assumption: unverified planning input requiring sensitivity or confirmation.
