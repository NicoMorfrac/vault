# Workflow - Change Costing

## Procedure

1. Identify baseline version, approved scope, requested change, requester, decision date, and schedule impact owner.
2. Separate removed, modified, added, reworked, wasted, expedited, cancelled, and unaffected work.
3. Record sunk/committed costs that cannot be recovered.
4. Request specialist estimates for technical, manufacturing, procurement, legal, documentation, installation, or schedule impacts.
5. Calculate the incremental cost against the unchanged baseline; do not re-price the entire project without instruction.
6. Apply approved risk/overhead/price policy separately and transparently.
7. State exclusions, validity, dependencies, and effect on cash/timing.
8. Return `CHANGE_COST_READY` for internal decision; never approve the change or communicate it to the client.

## Output

| Change element | Remove/add/rework | Quantity/rate | Incremental cost | Source | Schedule/dependency | Confidence |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|

Show baseline total, approved changes to date, proposed delta, revised cost scenario, and approval status separately.
