# Workflow - Actual Versus Estimate

## Data contract

Require baseline version/date, actual period, committed cost, open purchase/external commitments, timesheets/labour basis, invoices, currency, change orders, accruals, and remaining forecast.

## Procedure

1. Freeze the approved comparison baseline and current data timestamp.
2. Reconcile actual and committed source rows to totals.
3. Map actual, committed, accrued, and remaining forecast to the same WBS and cost categories as the baseline.
4. Separate approved change, scope growth, rate variance, quantity/hour variance, schedule effect, supplier variance, currency, rework, and data timing.
5. Calculate variance, remaining estimate, and estimate at completion only where inputs reconcile.
6. Flag unapproved changes and missing commitments; do not bury them in remaining forecast.
7. Distinguish cash paid, accounting cost, committed spend, and forecast.
8. Recommend owner actions and next review date without changing systems.

## Output

| WBS | Baseline | Actual | Committed | Forecast remaining | EAC | Variance | Cause/confidence |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|

Never infer profitability from incomplete actuals or compare incompatible periods/currencies.
