# Workflow - Estimate Calculation

## Procedure

1. Freeze the estimate version, date, currency, and approved calculation basis.
2. Normalise quantities, time units, currencies, and tax treatment.
3. Calculate each row as quantity x unit rate, or use a supported quoted lump sum.
4. Separate labour, bought-out items, external work, NRE/tooling, testing, logistics, travel/installation, approved indirect/overhead, and other categories.
5. Reconcile work-package subtotals to the base-cost total.
6. Apply exchange-rate conversion only from a dated approved source and retain original currency.
7. Apply CEO/user-approved overhead once and show its base.
8. Apply risk/contingency only through the risk workflow and show its base/method.
9. Keep recoverable taxes, cash timing, deposits, and financing exposure separate from cost unless approved accounting treatment says otherwise.
10. Calculate price scenarios only in the price workflow.
11. Show unpriced items and do not include them as zero without warning.

## Arithmetic controls

- All rows use explicit units.
- Subtotals reconcile to total.
- Rounding is applied only for presentation.
- Negative amounts require explanation.
- Zero values are distinguished from missing values.
- The estimate total states whether it includes overhead, contingency, tax, duty, freight, travel, and escalation.
