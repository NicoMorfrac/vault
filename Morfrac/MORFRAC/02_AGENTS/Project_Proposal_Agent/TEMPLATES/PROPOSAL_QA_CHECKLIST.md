# Proposal QA Checklist

## Identity and revision

- [ ] Correct project, client, proposal ID, version, date, language and confidentiality.
- [ ] Source register and version comparison complete.

## Scope and technical

- [ ] Approved scope and revision used.
- [ ] Deliverables, responsibilities, acceptance, assumptions and exclusions align.
- [ ] Technical/performance/compliance claims reviewed by accountable owner.

## Schedule and commercial

- [ ] Schedule source and dependencies identified.
- [ ] Price/options exactly match approved client-safe reference.
- [ ] Currency, tax, validity and payment wording sourced.
- [ ] No internal costs, margins, discount limits or supplier-confidential terms exposed.

## Legal and release

- [ ] Approved standard terms/revision or legal review evidenced.
- [ ] Deviations resolved or explicitly blocking.
- [ ] No unresolved client-visible placeholders.
- [ ] Save and release approvals are separate and valid.
- [ ] Human sender and channel identified; agent has not sent or signed.
