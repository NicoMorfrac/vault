# Proposal Structure Standard

Every proposal draft must contain:

- document control and draft status;
- executive summary tied to client need;
- scope, deliverables and boundaries;
- responsibilities and client inputs;
- acceptance basis;
- schedule, triggers, milestones and dependencies;
- approved price/options with currency and tax basis;
- assumptions and exclusions;
- change-control basis;
- approved terms reference or visible legal-review status;
- validity;
- appendices and related links.

The client draft must be understandable without internal notes. The internal review pack must make every source, decision and unresolved risk auditable.
