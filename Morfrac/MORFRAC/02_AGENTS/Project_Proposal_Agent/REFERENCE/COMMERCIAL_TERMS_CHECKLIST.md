# Commercial and Legal Terms Checklist

Review, source and route as applicable:

- offer validity;
- currency, VAT/tax and price adjustment;
- payment schedule, deposits and late payment;
- delivery basis, freight, duty and customs;
- client dependencies and delay effects;
- scope changes and cancellation;
- acceptance, rejection and remedy;
- warranty scope, start, duration and exclusions;
- liability, indemnity, insurance and consequential loss;
- confidentiality and publicity;
- background and project intellectual property/licensing;
- data protection/security;
- regulatory/export controls;
- force majeure;
- termination/suspension;
- governing law, jurisdiction and precedence.

Absence is a review item, not an invitation to invent terms.
