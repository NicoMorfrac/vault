# Workflow - Terms and Legal Review

This agent coordinates terms; it does not provide legal advice or approve clauses.

1. Identify the applicable approved MORFRAC standard terms and exact revision, if one exists.
2. Compare client-requested terms against the approved standard.
3. Record deviations covering payment, cancellation, delay, acceptance, warranty, liability, indemnity, insurance, confidentiality, intellectual property, data protection, export/customs, governing law, jurisdiction, termination, force majeure, and precedence.
4. Reproduce approved clauses without semantic rewriting.
5. Flag translations of legal clauses for review; never treat an automatic translation as legally equivalent.
6. Route bespoke, conflicting, absent, or high-risk terms to Legal Agent/human counsel and CEO.

If no approved standard or legal review exists, use `LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED` and placeholders. Do not invent protective wording and present it as approved.
