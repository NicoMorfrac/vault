# Workflow - Source and Revision Control

Create a source register before drafting.

| Topic | Source path/issue | Source owner | Revision/date | Approval evidence | Freshness | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Required topics include client request, scope, technical basis, deliverables, schedule, price/options, tax basis, payment, validity, warranty, legal terms, acceptance, and confidentiality.

Rules:

- Use stable identifiers and exact revisions.
- Compare document dates and explicit approval dates; latest does not automatically mean approved.
- Record discrepancies rather than merging contradictory values.
- Preserve the exact approved customer-facing total and currency/tax wording.
- A proposal revision must record every changed source.
