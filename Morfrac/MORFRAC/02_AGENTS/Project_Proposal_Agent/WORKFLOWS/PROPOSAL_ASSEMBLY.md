# Workflow - Proposal Assembly

Build the client draft using `TEMPLATES/CLIENT_PROPOSAL_DRAFT.md` and the internal pack using `TEMPLATES/INTERNAL_REVIEW_PACK.md`.

Client draft order:

1. document control and non-binding draft status;
2. executive summary;
3. client need and objectives;
4. proposed solution and scope;
5. deliverables and acceptance;
6. project organisation and responsibilities;
7. schedule and milestones;
8. price and options;
9. assumptions, dependencies, and exclusions;
10. commercial and legal terms by approved reference;
11. validity and acceptance/signature blocks for human completion;
12. appendices.

Never include source notes, internal rates, margin, discount authority, supplier terms, internal risk scoring, or private review comments in the client draft.
