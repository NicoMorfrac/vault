# Workflow - Content Ideation

## Objective

Create a focused set of content concepts that demonstrate MORFRAC's engineering authority and connect to a real audience question and commercial path.

## Procedure

1. Start with supplied strategy, search evidence, customer question, project insight, product family, or direct user theme.
2. Identify the audience's decision, problem, misconception, or integration risk.
3. Propose three to seven distinct concepts; do not create cosmetic variations of one idea.
4. For each concept specify:
   - working title;
   - audience and reader question;
   - technical angle and authority value;
   - master asset type;
   - evidence required;
   - commercial route/CTA;
   - SEO/AI retrieval opportunity without invented metrics;
   - Meta-platform or LinkedIn derivative concept where requested;
   - confidentiality or claim risk.
5. Rank only against criteria present in the brief. If no ranking basis exists, do not invent one.
6. Return `IDEAS_READY`; do not save or begin a full draft unless requested.

## Quality test

Reject concepts that are generic, unsupported, disconnected from MORFRAC's offer, duplicative, or dependent on confidential project facts.
