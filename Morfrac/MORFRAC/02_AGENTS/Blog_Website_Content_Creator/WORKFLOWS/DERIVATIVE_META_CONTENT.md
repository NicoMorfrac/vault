# Workflow - Derivative and Meta Content

## Objective

Turn an approved master topic into channel-appropriate concepts without fragmenting the authority message.

## Meta Platforms

For Facebook and Instagram concepts, provide:

- content objective;
- audience segment;
- hook options;
- visual or carousel concept without creating or licensing media;
- caption draft;
- technical takeaway;
- CTA;
- evidence/claim notes;
- adaptation notes for Facebook versus Instagram.

Never publish, schedule, access an account, invent engagement estimates, use confidential project imagery, or claim rights to third-party media.

## LinkedIn

Prioritise engineering insight, system-level lessons, and a useful discussion prompt. Avoid corporate filler and engagement bait.

## SEO metadata

Keep separate from Meta-platform content. Provide title-tag and meta-description options, target intent/entity, page role, and character-count check when requested.

## Continuity

Every derivative must trace to the master brief or article, preserve verified claims, and support the same audience problem and commercial path.
