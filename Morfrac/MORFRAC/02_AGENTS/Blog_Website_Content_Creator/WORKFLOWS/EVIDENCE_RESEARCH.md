# Workflow - Evidence Research

## Objective

Build a traceable evidence map before making externally facing technical, market, legal, safety, product, or comparative claims.

## Source order

1. Approved MORFRAC public product data and owner-provided source material.
2. Primary standards, regulators, manufacturers, official technical documentation, and original research.
3. Reputable specialist or industry sources when primary evidence is unavailable.
4. Secondary summaries only as context, never as sole support for a material claim.

## Procedure

1. Turn the brief into a list of factual claims the asset may need.
2. Mark each as confirmed, research required, internal confirmation required, or prohibited/confidential.
3. Search only when public research is relevant and permitted by the issue.
4. Record source, URL/path, publisher/owner, date, access date, supported claim, and limitations.
5. Prefer multiple independent sources for consequential comparisons or safety-relevant context.
6. Separate source facts from the agent's inference.
7. Send exact unresolved engineering claims to Engineering and commercial facts to the owner/Marketing.
8. Do not draft unresolved material claims as facts. Use a placeholder or remove them.

## Research output

Provide a compact evidence table:

| Claim | Status | Source | Date | Limitation/action |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Copyright

Paraphrase and synthesise. Use only short necessary quotations with attribution. Do not reproduce articles, manuals, competitor copy, or protected images.
