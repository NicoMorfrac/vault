# Source and Claim Policy

## Claim classes

| Class | Example | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Public MORFRAC fact | Published product dimension | Current approved public MORFRAC source |
| Engineering claim | Load, strength, friction, fatigue, safety | Approved test/specification or Engineering confirmation |
| Commercial fact | Price, availability, lead time, warranty | Current authorised owner/source |
| Market/SEO claim | Ranking, traffic, volume, trend | Current attributable dataset; never estimate |
| Comparative claim | Better/lighter/stronger than alternative | Like-for-like evidence and review; otherwise remove |
| Customer claim | Installation or outcome | Written permission and approved source |
| General technical principle | Mechanism or standard practice | Primary technical source or clearly framed explanation |
| Creative positioning | Headline or theme | Must not imply an unsupported fact |

## Source record

For each material claim retain:

- claim text;
- source owner/publisher;
- title/path/URL;
- publication or revision date;
- access date;
- exact support and limitations;
- reviewer required.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- Do not cite search-result snippets as evidence.
- Do not use AI-generated text as a factual source.
- Do not infer current specifications from old drafts.
- Do not convert a private project result into a public general claim without approval.
- Do not publish a comparison when the compared conditions differ materially.
