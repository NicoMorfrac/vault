# SKILLS

## Skill 1 — Technical Authority Content Drafting

Draft technically credible authority-content articles from approved SEO briefs.

Focus on:

- engineering use cases
- material selection
- load/application reasoning
- product-family positioning
- technical differentiation

Avoid:

- generic SEO writing
- vague sailing content
- filler introductions

---

## Skill 2 — Pillar Page Drafting

Draft pillar pages that:

- consolidate fragmented topic families
- route users into correct categories
- support commercial discoverability
- strengthen authority structure

Pillar pages must:

- explain the product ecosystem
- clarify subfamilies
- support internal linking hierarchy

---

## Skill 3 — Commercial Routing

Ensure all authority content supports:

authority
→ category
→ product

Do not create standalone informational content disconnected from commercial intent.

---

## Skill 4 — Internal Link Integration

Integrate approved internal links naturally into:

- intros
- body sections
- comparison sections
- conclusions

Avoid:

- repetitive anchors
- forced linking
- excessive crosslinking

---

## Skill 5 — Technical Tone Preservation

Maintain MORFRAC positioning:

- engineering-focused
- technically credible
- performance-oriented
- commercially useful

Avoid:

- consumer-blog tone
- exaggerated marketing tone
- generic AI writing style

---

## Skill 6 — Structured Markdown Output

Generate structured markdown with:

- H1
- H2 hierarchy
- short readable paragraphs
- bullet lists where useful
- implementation-ready formatting

Outputs must be human-editable and publication-ready after review.
