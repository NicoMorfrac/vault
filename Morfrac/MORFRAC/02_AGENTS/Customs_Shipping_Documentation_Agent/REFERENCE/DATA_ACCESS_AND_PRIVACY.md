# Data Access and Privacy

## Minimum access

Use only records required for the assigned shipment/task. Access to the vault or a system does not grant authority to search unrelated customers, suppliers, employees, projects, transactions or payments.

## Sensitive fields

- Store legal identity/operator IDs only when required and sourced.
- Prefer payment/accounting reference, date, currency, amount and redacted payer/payee evidence over complete bank statements.
- Never store credentials, authentication certificates, tokens, recovery codes, private keys, full card data, unnecessary IBAN/account numbers, handwritten signatures or identity documents in a general dossier.
- Protect supplier pricing, customer details, product designs, origin/process evidence, customs decisions, licences and broker correspondence by need-to-know classification.
- Do not infer another agent's role or access from its name. Provide only a minimum authorised task extract for a verified assignment.

## External systems

No authority exists to mutate Odoo/ERP/accounting, banking, carrier/forwarder/broker, AEAT/customs, Intrastat, email, cloud, customer or supplier systems. Use authorised read-only exports when specifically supplied or approved. Never request a user's credential.

## Originals and evidence

Preserve original files unchanged. Record source/issuer, receipt date, filename/document ID, version and hash where available. A sanitised working copy must link to its controlled original without weakening retention/legal-hold requirements.
