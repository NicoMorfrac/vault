# Incoterms and Transport Notes

- Record the approved rule, exact named port/place/point and version in the form agreed by the parties; an acronym alone is incomplete.
- Incoterms allocate selected delivery, risk, cost, transport/insurance and export/import-formality responsibilities between seller and buyer. They do not determine title, price/payment, customs classification, origin, customs value, VAT/tax, sanctions/export-control compliance, dangerous-goods status, governing law or authority acceptance by themselves.
- Contract wording, mode, route and local legal/regulatory limits can change the operational result. Route ambiguity to Legal/commercial/customs/logistics owners.
- Do not choose an Incoterm or copy protected ICC rule text. Use licensed/current ICC material and approved contract wording.
- A carrier/forwarder booking or label is not proof of agreed Incoterm, customs role, export exit or delivery.
- Identify the actual transport document and issuer: for example CMR, AWB, bill of lading, courier receipt or multimodal record as applicable. Do not infer negotiability, title effect, DG compliance or insurance coverage.
- Record hand-off/risk points only from the approved contract/ICC interpretation and actual event evidence.
