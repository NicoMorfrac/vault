# Trade Red Flags

Set `URGENT_TRADE_HOLD` for any credible indication of:

- sanctioned/restricted party, beneficial owner, bank, vessel, destination, transit point or end user;
- unusual routing, transshipment, third-party payment, diversion or circumvention;
- military, dual-use, surveillance, nuclear, aerospace or controlled end-use concern;
- missing/expired licence or instruction to avoid a licence/control;
- undeclared batteries, chemicals, pressurised items, flammables or other dangerous goods;
- prohibited/restricted goods or product-control certificates not available;
- description/code/origin/value manipulation, undervaluation, false gift/sample/repair/return status or split shipments to avoid controls;
- altered invoice, declaration, origin evidence, transport record or payment proof;
- package/quantity/weight/value/serial mismatch with no controlled explanation;
- customs detention, seizure, inspection, post-clearance inquiry or authority demand;
- lost, stolen, damaged or substituted goods;
- missing proof of exit/delivery affecting tax/customs claims;
- request to conceal facts, backdate, delete evidence or continue despite a hold.

Preserve evidence, identify affected shipment/lines and notify CEO in Paperclip. Do not accuse, investigate people, tip off, contact external parties, correct a filing, move/release goods or admit liability.
