# Workflow - Trade Master Library and Retention

Reusable controlled records include:

- MORFRAC legal-entity/operator/EORI/VAT data approved by the owner;
- broker/carrier/forwarder contacts and representation evidence;
- approved product classification/origin decisions with exact applicability;
- standard document templates and terminology;
- shipment register fields, naming, dossier structure and access classes;
- retention schedule with Legal/accounting/customs owner decisions;
- official-source baseline and review dates.

Before any promotion, show current/proposed values, source/reviewer, effective/review/expiry dates, affected shipments/documents, migration and exact paths. Require `APPROVE TRADE MASTER <Issue-ID>`.

Never silently convert a shipment fact, candidate code, supplier declaration or broker practice into master data. Apply the longest applicable authorised retention rule and a legal hold where directed. The agent does not delete records.
