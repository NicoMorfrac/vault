# Workflow - Origin and Preference

Keep these separate:

- country of dispatch/export;
- non-preferential origin;
- preferential origin under a named arrangement;
- country of manufacture/processing and component origins;
- invoice/marketing “made in” statements.

Build a line-level evidence matrix with product-specific rule/reference, tariff classification dependency, materials/process evidence, direct transport/non-alteration evidence where applicable, supplier declarations and validity dates. Route uncertainty to a qualified customs/origin owner and consider BOI review.

Do not infer origin from supplier address, purchase country, shipment route, final assembly location or a previous preference claim. Do not draft or issue an origin declaration/certificate without approved eligibility, wording and signatory authority.
