# Workflow - Intra-EU, Special Territories and Intrastat Screen

Determine only the review path, not the final tax/statistical conclusion:

1. Record actual dispatch/destination territories and Union-goods status evidence.
2. Identify special customs/fiscal territories, triangulation/chain transactions, installation/assembly, call-off stock, repairs/returns or other complexities.
3. Obtain VAT/VIES and intra-Community treatment from the accounting/Legal owner.
4. Screen whether the company is an Intrastat information provider for the relevant flow/period and obtain the current threshold/notification from AEAT/accounting.
5. Reconcile Intrastat data to physical movement and accounting records.

Intra-EU movement is not automatically “no documentation.” EORI and VIES are not the same. The agent never files Intrastat.
