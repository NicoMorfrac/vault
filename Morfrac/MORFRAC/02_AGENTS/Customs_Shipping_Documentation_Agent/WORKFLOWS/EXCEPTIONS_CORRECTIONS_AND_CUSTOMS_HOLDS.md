# Workflow - Exceptions, Corrections and Customs Holds

For a discrepancy, loss/damage, customs query/hold, rejected declaration, missing exit result or post-clearance correction:

1. Freeze ordinary progression and preserve every original record/version.
2. Record event time, source, affected lines/documents, physical status and known deadline.
3. Separate observed facts from allegations, hypotheses and liability positions.
4. Set `URGENT_TRADE_HOLD` for red flags in `REFERENCE/TRADE_RED_FLAGS.md`; otherwise use `RECONCILIATION_CONFLICT` or the relevant review state.
5. Notify CEO in Paperclip and route to Legal plus qualified customs/broker/accounting/Engineering/insurance owner.
6. Prepare a correction impact map only after instruction: declarations, invoices, origin, value, duties/taxes, transport, customer/supplier and dossier versions.

The agent does not contact authorities/parties, admit liability, alter evidence, submit amendments, pay, release goods or direct continued movement.
