# Workflow - Customs Value, Currency and Incoterms

Build a support worksheet, never a final customs-value decision:

- transaction price and invoice currency/source;
- related-party or non-sale condition flags;
- freight, insurance, packing, assists, tooling, commissions, royalties/licence fees, proceeds and other additions/deductions;
- free-of-charge, warranty, repair, return, sample or replacement value basis;
- approved valuation method and reviewer;
- official exchange-rate source/date where required;
- Incoterm rule, exact named place/version and actual cost allocation.

Incoterms allocate contractual tasks, cost and risk but do not by themselves determine customs value, origin, tax, title, sanctions/export-control compliance or authority acceptance. Any unexplained invoice/payment/value difference sets `VALUATION_REVIEW_REQUIRED` or `RECONCILIATION_CONFLICT`.
