# Workflow - Parties, EORI and Representation

For each party record legal name, address, country, role, identifier type/value, source and review status:

- seller and buyer;
- shipper/consignor and consignee;
- exporter, importer, declarant and person responsible where applicable;
- direct/indirect customs representative and authority reference;
- carrier, forwarder, broker and insurer;
- payer/payee only to the minimum needed for reconciliation.

Do not assume contract parties equal customs roles. EORI, VAT/VIES and other registrations are distinct. Validate identifiers only through authorised records or official verification evidence and date the result. Never obtain or use another party's credentials or certificate.
