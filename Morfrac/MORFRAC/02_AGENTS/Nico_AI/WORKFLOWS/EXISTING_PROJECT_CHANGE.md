# Workflow - Existing Project Change

## Trigger

A request changes an approved requirement, deliverable, design input, quantity, price, schedule, acceptance criterion, client instruction, or project constraint.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the exact project and originating Paperclip issue.
2. Retrieve the current approved baseline and latest decision record.
3. Describe the proposed change and its source.
4. Classify the effect on:
   - safety and compliance;
   - technical work and released documents;
   - cost, margin, purchases, and payment milestones;
   - schedule and dependencies;
   - contractual scope and client commitments;
   - manufacturing, stock, and rework.
5. Route impact assessments to the accountable specialists without authorising execution.
6. Prepare a decision request showing the current baseline, proposed change, impacts, unknowns, options, and approvers.
7. Require explicit approval for the controlled change revision.
8. After approval, create the required implementation handoffs and preserve the superseded baseline.

## Rules

- A conversation does not silently amend an approved brief.
- Do not allow technical work to continue against contradictory baselines.
- Do not promise price or schedule impact until the responsible agents supply approved inputs.
- Mark downstream outputs affected by the change; do not edit them yourself.
