\# Workflow - Existing Project Change



\## Trigger



Use this workflow when a request materially changes an approved project baseline, such as:



\* requirements;

\* deliverables;

\* design inputs;

\* quantity;

\* price or commercial terms;

\* schedule;

\* acceptance criteria;

\* client instructions;

\* project constraints.



Minor clarifications that do not change the approved baseline do not require this workflow.



\---



\## Procedure



1\. Identify the project and current approved baseline.



2\. Define the proposed change and its source.



3\. Identify affected areas, as relevant:



&#x20;  \* technical or safety;

&#x20;  \* cost or procurement;

&#x20;  \* schedule;

&#x20;  \* contractual/commercial;

&#x20;  \* manufacturing or rework;

&#x20;  \* released documents or deliverables.



4\. Route only the required impact assessments to the accountable specialists.



5\. Continue unaffected project work.



6\. Present Nico with:



&#x20;  \* current baseline;

&#x20;  \* proposed change;

&#x20;  \* material impacts;

&#x20;  \* important unknowns;

&#x20;  \* recommendation or options;

&#x20;  \* decision required.



7\. Obtain human approval before implementing a material controlled change.



8\. After approval:



&#x20;  \* route the required implementation work;

&#x20;  \* preserve traceability to the superseded baseline;

&#x20;  \* update affected project records through the appropriate owner.



\---



\## Rules



\* A conversation must not silently change an approved baseline.

\* Do not continue affected work against contradictory requirements.

\* Unaffected work should continue where practical.

\* Do not invent price, schedule or technical consequences.

\* Assessment of a change does not itself authorise implementation.

\* Human approval may be expressed in clear natural language unless an underlying system technically requires exact syntax.
