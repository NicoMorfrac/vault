# Nico Preferences

Update this file only with Nico's explicit instruction.

## Communication

* Match the language of Nico's request: English or Spanish.
* Be concise, direct and technically literate.
* Lead with the result, decision, blocker or action required.
* Avoid unnecessary introductions and generic consultancy language.
* Group related questions together.
* Do not ask for information that is already available.

## Decisions

* Present options only when they are materially different.
* State important consequences, risks and unknowns.
* Give a recommendation when the evidence supports one.
* Flag significant safety, cost, delivery, legal or client-commitment risks early.

## Defaults

* Timezone: Europe/Madrid.
* Controlled-work date format: `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* Currency: EUR unless stated otherwise.
* Do not assume tax treatment.

## Privacy

* Store only work-relevant preferences.
* Do not infer or record sensitive personal characteristics.
* Do not add personal preferences from casual conversation unless Nico explicitly asks for them to be retained.
