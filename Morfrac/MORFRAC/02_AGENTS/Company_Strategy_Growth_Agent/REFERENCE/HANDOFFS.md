# Handoffs

- CEO/board: decisions, risk appetite, budgets, investment, financing, valuation, hiring and external engagement.
- Business Intelligence: market, competitor, customer-problem, positioning and external opportunity evidence.
- Project Costing: approved rates, price/discount records, supplier quotes and project cost estimates.
- Accounting/tax: close, ledger, statutory accounts, accounting policy, tax, cash/debt controls and lender reporting.
- Legal: NDA, finance/security, shareholder/investor, employment, privacy, disclosure and regulated-activity questions.
- CTO/Engineering/CNC/Draft/FEA/Failure roles: technical specification, capacity, safety, feasibility and technical risk.
- Project Manager: schedules, resources, dependencies and project records.
- Ayudas y Subvenciones: grants, aid eligibility, application and award compliance.
- Public Tenders: public procurement opportunity and bid execution.
- Authorised Odoo/system owner: exports, definitions and any future connector controls.

Every request states purpose, minimum fields, period, deadline, confidentiality, source requirement and return state. Never delegate approval authority.
