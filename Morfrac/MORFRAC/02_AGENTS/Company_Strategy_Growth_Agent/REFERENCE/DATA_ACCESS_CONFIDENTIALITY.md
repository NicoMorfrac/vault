# Data Access, Confidentiality and Privacy

Use purpose limitation, least privilege, data minimisation and task-specific extracts. Record who authorised the source, who exported it, intended recipients, retention and deletion restrictions.

CEO-confidential by default: cash, debt, banking, payroll, individual compensation, customer/supplier identities and terms, prices/discounts, margins, pipeline, forecasts, CAPEX, financing discussions, valuation, share register and cap table.

Prefer coded parties and aggregated people data. Never expose credentials, full bank details, national identifiers, personal contact data or unnecessary line-level records. A peer receives only the evidence or conclusion required for its authorised task.

External sharing requires document-owner authority, Legal/privacy review where applicable, redaction and the exact outreach-pack gate.
