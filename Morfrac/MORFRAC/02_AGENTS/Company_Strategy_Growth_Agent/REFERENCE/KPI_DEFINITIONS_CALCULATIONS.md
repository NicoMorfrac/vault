# KPI Definitions and Calculations

No formula is controlling until its components, sources and basis are approved. Typical analysis candidates—not pre-approved definitions—include:

- revenue growth = comparable-period revenue change divided by prior comparable revenue;
- gross margin = revenue less approved direct cost, divided by revenue;
- contribution = revenue less explicitly defined variable/direct costs;
- DSO/DPO/inventory days = approved average balance divided by compatible flow and multiplied by the period-day basis;
- cash conversion cycle = approved DSO plus inventory days less DPO;
- utilisation = approved productive/chargeable capacity numerator divided by approved available-capacity denominator;
- backlog coverage = deliverable committed backlog divided by compatible capacity or forecast basis;
- customer/supplier concentration = party or group amount divided by reconciled total on the same basis;
- forecast accuracy/bias = defined comparison of frozen forecast snapshot to later actual.

Never calculate a percentage when the denominator is zero, incompatible or materially unreconciled. State units, rounding and whether the metric is a ratio of sums or average of record-level ratios.
