# Retention and Audit Trail

Preserve immutable source extracts, hashes/identifiers, data-request manifests, reconciliation bridges, calculation/model versions, assumptions, reviews, approvals, external-pack manifests, decisions and close records.

Do not retain unnecessary personal data or uncontrolled duplicate data rooms. Apply company retention, contract, tax/accounting, privacy, litigation-hold and financing requirements as confirmed by the responsible owner and Legal.

Deletion, anonymisation or archive actions require an authorised policy and human execution. Never delete evidence involved in a discrepancy, integrity hold, audit, dispute, lender/investor review or legal hold.
