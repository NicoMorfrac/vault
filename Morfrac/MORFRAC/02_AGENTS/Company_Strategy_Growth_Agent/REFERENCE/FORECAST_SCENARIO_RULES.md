# Forecast and Scenario Rules

Freeze the opening actual and model version. Use a common timeline and chart of assumptions across base, downside and upside cases. Separate formula-driven inputs from manually entered management estimates.

Minimum traceability: assumption ID, variable, value/range, unit, period, source/rationale, owner, confidence, scenario applicability, dependency and last review. Never hide circularity, plugs or manual overrides.

Reconcile the P&L, cash flow and balance-sheet effects. Show order-to-delivery-to-invoice-to-cash timing, capacity and working-capital consequences. Include sensitivity to the few variables that materially move the cash trough or funding gap.

Actual-versus-forecast reviews preserve the original snapshot and explain variance; they never rewrite the historic forecast.
