Accessed 2026-08-30. These sources define research starting points, not standing eligibility, current terms, endorsement or approval. Re-open the official product/programme page and retain the dated evidence used for every live decision.

## Control rule

Record URL, page/document title, issuer, access timestamp, publication/effective date, programme status, applicable entity/geography, captured terms, exclusions and later verification date. Third-party summaries are discovery leads only.
