# Investment Decision Rules

An investment case must identify the constraint and causal mechanism, not begin with a preferred asset. Include no-action, staged/pilot and non-purchase alternatives when credible.

Cash flows include acquisition, installation, integration, downtime, training, tooling, maintenance, consumables, financing, working capital, tax/accounting treatment, decommissioning and residual value as applicable. Capacity benefits include yield, uptime, changeover, quality, labour and ramp assumptions.

Evaluate downside demand and implementation delay. Identify irreversible commitments, safety/compliance dependencies, supplier concentration, data/system requirements and exit. Financial metrics never replace technical validation or CEO/board approval.
