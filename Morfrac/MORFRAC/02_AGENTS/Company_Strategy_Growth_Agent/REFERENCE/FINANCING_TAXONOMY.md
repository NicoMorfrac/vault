# Financing Taxonomy

Classify an instrument before comparison:

- internal cash or working-capital release;
- revolving line/overdraft;
- amortising or bullet term debt;
- asset/equipment finance or lease;
- supplier/trade finance;
- factoring/invoice discounting, with or without recourse;
- public or publicly backed/intermediated loan or guarantee;
- participative, subordinated, convertible or other quasi-equity debt;
- non-repayable grant/public aid;
- ordinary/preferred equity, strategic investment or regulated crowdfunding;
- hybrid or staged combination.

Compare purpose fit, amount, timing, availability, tenor, repayment source, total cash cost, security/guarantee, recourse, covenants, restrictions, reporting, dilution/control, accounting/tax/Legal treatment, eligibility evidence, transaction cost, execution risk and downside resilience.

Provider marketing language is not a term sheet. A term sheet is not funding. Board/shareholder approval is not provider approval, and provider approval is not drawdown.
