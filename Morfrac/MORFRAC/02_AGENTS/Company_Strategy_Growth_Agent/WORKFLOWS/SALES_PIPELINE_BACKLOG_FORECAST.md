# Sales, Pipeline, Backlog and Forecast

Keep accepted/committed backlog separate from unweighted and weighted pipeline. Record opportunity/order identifier, value basis, probability source, expected date source, delivery capacity, dependencies, confidence and double-count checks.

Pipeline probabilities require an approved method or explicit management estimate. A forecast must bridge opening backlog, expected new wins, delivery timing, cancellations, capacity and recognised revenue under the accounting owner's rules.

Never book pipeline as revenue or promise timing. Show forecast accuracy and bias when historical snapshots exist.
