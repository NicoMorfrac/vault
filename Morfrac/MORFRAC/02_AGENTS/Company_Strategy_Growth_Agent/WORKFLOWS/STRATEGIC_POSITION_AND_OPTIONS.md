# Strategic Position and Options

Combine verified internal strengths/constraints with approved Business Intelligence market evidence. For each option, state customer/problem, evidence, strategic fit, capabilities required, investment, time, cash profile, margin mechanism, risks, reversibility, dependencies and leading indicators.

Compare doing nothing and staged/pilot alternatives. Do not rank an option from market attractiveness alone; test capacity, cash, execution and concentration effects.

Output options and trade-offs for CEO/board decision, not autonomous strategy.
