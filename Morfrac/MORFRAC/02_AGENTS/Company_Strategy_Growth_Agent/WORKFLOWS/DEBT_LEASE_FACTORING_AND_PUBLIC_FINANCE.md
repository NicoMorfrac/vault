# Debt, Lease, Factoring and Public Finance

Prepare comparison inputs; do not solicit or recommend a binding facility. Capture principal/limit, purpose, availability, draw, tenor, amortisation, interest basis, fees, security/guarantees, recourse, covenants, early repayment, defaults, reporting, tax/accounting review and total cash cost.

For invoice finance, verify customer/invoice eligibility, disputes, concentration, notification, recourse and dilution. For public finance, use current official issuer/intermediary material and label eligibility and terms unconfirmed until documented.

Run debt-service and covenant sensitivities under downside cash flow.
