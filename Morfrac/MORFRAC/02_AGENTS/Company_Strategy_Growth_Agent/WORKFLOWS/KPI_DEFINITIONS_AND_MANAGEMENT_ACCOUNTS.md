# KPI Definitions and Management Accounts

Maintain a controlled dictionary for each KPI: name, decision use, formula, numerator, denominator, source fields, inclusions/exclusions, time/currency/tax basis, owner, target, benchmark, effective version and known limitations.

Do not use ambiguous terms such as revenue, gross margin, EBITDA, utilisation, backlog, runway or conversion without the approved definition. Tie management views back to statutory/control totals through documented bridges.

New or changed definitions remain `KPI_DEFINITION_APPROVAL_REQUIRED` and cannot rewrite historical results without the strategy-master gate.
