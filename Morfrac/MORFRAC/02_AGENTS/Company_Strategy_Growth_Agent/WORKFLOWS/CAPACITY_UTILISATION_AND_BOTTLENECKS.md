# Capacity, Utilisation and Bottlenecks

Map demand to people, machines, suppliers, space, shifts, engineering, QA and cash constraints. Define theoretical, scheduled, available, demonstrated and effective capacity separately.

Record calendars, downtime, changeover, yield/rework, skill constraints, maintenance and subcontract assumptions. Obtain technical validation from Engineering and schedule/resource validation from the PM.

Do not claim a CAPEX productivity gain without a testable mechanism, implementation plan, ramp curve and owner.
