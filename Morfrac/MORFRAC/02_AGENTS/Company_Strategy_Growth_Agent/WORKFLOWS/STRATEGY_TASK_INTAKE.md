# Strategy Task Intake

Set `STRATEGY_TASK_INTAKE_REQUIRED` until requester, decision owner, exact question, deadline, legal entity/company perimeter, period, currency, materiality, confidentiality, output audience and decision type are known.

Classify the request as company diagnosis, KPI/reporting, commercial performance, working capital, capacity, strategic option, growth scenario, investment, financing, due diligence or periodic review. Record what decision the output must support and what the agent is explicitly not authorised to do.

Do not request a company-wide dump when a narrower extract answers the question.
