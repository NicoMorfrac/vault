# Growth Scenario Modelling

Build a base, downside and upside case from a common approved opening position. For each case, list price, volume, mix, win rate, timing, capacity, hiring, cost, working-capital, CAPEX, tax and financing assumptions.

Show monthly or decision-appropriate phasing, P&L bridge, cash flow, balance-sheet effects, cash trough, funding gap, headroom, break-even, sensitivities and trigger conditions. Preserve formulas and source lineage.

Scenarios are conditional planning tools, never predictions or approved budgets.
