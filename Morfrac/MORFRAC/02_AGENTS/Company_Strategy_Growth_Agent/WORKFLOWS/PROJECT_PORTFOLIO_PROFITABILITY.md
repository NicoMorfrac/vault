# Project Portfolio Profitability

Link each project to the authoritative project ID, approved scope, contract/order, recognised revenue, committed cost, incurred cost, estimated cost to complete, margin basis, schedule status, variations and risk owner.

Separate actual cost, committed cost and estimate-to-complete. Do not infer completion or margin from invoicing alone. Escalate missing timesheets, purchasing, WIP or change approvals to the responsible owner.

Report profitability ranges when data quality or allocation is uncertain.
