# Investment, CAPEX and Return Analysis

Define the problem before the asset. Compare repair, outsource, lease, buy, staged investment and no-action cases when applicable.

Record technical specification owner, capacity mechanism, installation/ramp, downtime, training, maintenance, consumables, tooling, space, safety/compliance, working capital, residual value and exit assumptions. Show cash flows and sensitivity rather than a single ROI number.

Use `CAPACITY_INVESTMENT_REVIEW_REQUIRED` until Engineering, Costing, PM, Accounting/Tax and Legal reviews appropriate to the case are complete. The investment gate authorises planning treatment only.
