# Company and Decision Baseline

Create a versioned baseline containing legal entities, operating sites, reporting currency, fiscal/calendar periods, ownership/decision authorities, approved accounting period, business lines, products/services, active projects, major capacity resources and known constraints.

Separate verified facts from management descriptions. Record source, owner, effective date and confidence for each item. Conflicts or an open accounting close keep the affected work in `COMPANY_BASELINE_REQUIRED` or `ACCOUNTING_CLOSE_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.

Use the baseline gate before treating the package as the controlling basis for analysis.
