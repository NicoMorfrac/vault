# Revenue and Margin Segmentation

Analyse revenue, direct cost, contribution and gross margin only where approved dimensions and cost allocation exist. Possible dimensions include product, service, customer, project, channel, geography and period.

Show unallocated costs separately. Identify intercompany, credits, returns, discounts, rebates, non-recurring items and incomplete jobs. Use Project Costing-approved rates and commercial records; do not reconstruct or override them.

Never present margin estimates as closed actuals.
