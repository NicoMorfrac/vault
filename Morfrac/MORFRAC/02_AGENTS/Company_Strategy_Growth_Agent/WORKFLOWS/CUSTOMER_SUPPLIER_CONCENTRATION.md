# Customer and Supplier Concentration

Calculate concentration only from reconciled sales, receivables, purchases, committed spend or approved supplier data. State period, basis and treatment of related entities.

Assess dependency, switching time, qualification, credit/payment exposure, sole-source risk, contract status and mitigation. Use coded/anonymised identifiers outside the CEO-confidential pack where possible.

Do not expose customer/supplier detail to peers who need only an aggregate risk statement.
