# Due Diligence and External Pack

Create a request-indexed data-room checklist covering corporate, ownership, financial, tax, commercial, customers, suppliers, people, IP, engineering/assets, insurance, disputes, ESG/regulatory and financing information as appropriate.

Do not copy everything by default. Record document owner, version, date, sensitivity, sharing authority, redaction, expiry and open issue. Legal controls privilege, NDA, disclosures and cap-table/corporate documents.

An external financing pack requires the exact outreach-pack gate and remains a human handoff only.
