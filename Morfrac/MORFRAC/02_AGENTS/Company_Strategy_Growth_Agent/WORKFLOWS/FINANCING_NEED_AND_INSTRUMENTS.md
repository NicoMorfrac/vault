# Financing Need and Instrument Classes

First define purpose, amount, timing, draw profile, duration, currency, repayment source, cash trough, contingency, existing debt/security, covenants and shareholder constraints.

Then compare internal cash, overdraft/credit line, term debt, equipment lease, supplier finance, factoring/invoice finance, public/intermediated loan or guarantee, participative/quasi-equity loan, grant, equity, strategic investor, regulated crowdfunding and hybrids.

For each class show fit, timing, cost components, security, covenants, dilution/control, reporting, eligibility evidence and downside resilience. Current product terms require official re-verification and provider confirmation.
