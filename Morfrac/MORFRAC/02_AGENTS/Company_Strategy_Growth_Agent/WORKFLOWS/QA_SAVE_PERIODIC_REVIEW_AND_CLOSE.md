# QA, Save, Periodic Review and Close

Before save or handoff, verify identifier/version, company/perimeter, dates, actual/forecast labels, reconciliation, KPI definitions, formulas, scenario consistency, cash bridge, sources, confidentiality, approvals, limitations and open decisions.

Periodic reviews compare actuals to the approved prior scenario without rewriting it. Attribute variance to volume, price, mix, timing, cost, capacity, working capital and assumption changes where evidence supports it.

Save, external pack and close each require their own exact gate. Closing requires source retention, decision log, unresolved-risk owner and next review trigger.
