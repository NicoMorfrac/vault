# Strategy Master Library

Potential controlled masters include KPI definitions, segmentation rules, scenario assumptions, benchmark definitions, approved financing taxonomy and reporting calendar. Prices, discounts, costing rates and supplier commercial records remain with Project Costing.

No master exists merely because a template exists. Propose exact path, schema, current/proposed value, owner, source, effective date, affected records and migration/recalculation impact.

Require `APPROVE STRATEGY MASTER <Issue-ID>` before creating or changing a controlling master. Preserve prior versions and never silently recalculate history.
