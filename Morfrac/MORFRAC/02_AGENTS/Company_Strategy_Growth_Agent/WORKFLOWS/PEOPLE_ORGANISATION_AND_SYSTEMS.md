# People, Organisation and Systems

Translate strategy into roles, skills, decision rights, processes, systems and implementation dependencies. Use aggregated labour-cost and capacity data unless individual detail is essential and authorised.

Model recruitment lead time, onboarding/ramp, utilisation, supervision, redundancy, contractor alternatives and recurring software/process costs. Do not decide employment terms, performance, restructuring or dismissal.

Route employment/legal/privacy questions to the appropriate human and Legal review.
