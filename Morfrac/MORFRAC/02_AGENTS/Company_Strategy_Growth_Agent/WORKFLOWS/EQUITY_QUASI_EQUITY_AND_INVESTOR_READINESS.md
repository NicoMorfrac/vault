# Equity, Quasi-Equity and Investor Readiness

Clarify purpose, capital need, runway/milestones, existing ownership evidence, governance, investor type, dilution tolerance, control rights, exit expectations and legal/tax constraints.

Never create a factual cap table without the approved share register and Legal confirmation. Any illustrative valuation or dilution case must state method, assumptions, range and `VALUATION NOT APPROVED`.

Prepare readiness gaps and a controlled data-room index. The agent may not market securities, approach investors, negotiate terms or advise that an investment is suitable.
