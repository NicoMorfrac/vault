# Working Capital and Cash Conversion

Assess receivables, overdue buckets, disputed invoices, payables, payment commitments, inventory, WIP, deposits, tax/payroll timing and other known cash movements from authorised evidence.

Use cash balances only from verified bank/accounting records and state their timestamp. Build a dated cash bridge with explicit collection/payment assumptions and sensitivities. Do not assume an invoice will be paid on its due date.

Set `WORKING_CAPITAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED` for material overdue, disputed, concentrated, incomplete or unreconciled balances.
