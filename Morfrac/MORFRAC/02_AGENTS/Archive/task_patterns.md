# SEO AGENT TASK PATTERNS

## PURPOSE

Task patterns define:
- how the SEO agent evaluates information
- how priorities are determined
- how recommendations are ranked
- how low-value SEO noise is filtered out

The SEO agent must operate as a commercial intelligence system, not a generic SEO assistant.

---

# TASK PATTERN — TECHNICAL SEO AUDIT

## Inputs

Required:
- crawl CSV
- crawl audit
- Search Console analysis

Optional:
- GA4 report
- strategic intelligence report

---

## Evaluation Sequence

1. Identify high-priority pages
2. Remove low-value/system noise
3. Detect structural SEO issues
4. Detect metadata weaknesses
5. Detect discoverability bottlenecks
6. Detect authority fragmentation
7. Prioritize by commercial leverage

---

## Prioritization Rules

Highest priority:
- product/category pages
- technical landing pages
- high-impression low-CTR pages
- near-page-one pages
- authority pages
- commercially relevant searches

Lowest priority:
- legal pages
- archives
- tag pages
- utility pages
- system pages

---

## Expected Outputs

- prioritized issue list
- affected page groups
- commercial interpretation
- implementation difficulty
- expected SEO impact
- authority implications

---

# TASK PATTERN — CTR IMPROVEMENT ANALYSIS

## Objective

Identify pages where:
- visibility exists
- clicks underperform

Focus:
- title weakness
- poor metadata
- weak search alignment
- unclear authority positioning

---

## Signals

Important:
- high impressions
- low CTR
- rankings between positions 4–15
- commercially relevant queries

Ignore:
- low-intent impressions
- weak commercial terms
- irrelevant geographies

---

## Expected Outputs

For each opportunity:
- page
- query/topic
- likely CTR problem
- recommendation
- expected impact
- confidence level

---

# TASK PATTERN — AUTHORITY POSITIONING

## Objective

Strengthen MORFRAC’s positioning as:
- technical authority
- engineering-focused manufacturer
- systems-level problem solver

---

## High-Value Authority Themes

Focus on:
- load paths
- textile interfaces
- friction reduction
- furling systems
- lightweight structures
- offshore reliability
- engineering integration
- failure prevention
- structural efficiency

Avoid:
- generic sailing content
- lifestyle topics
- broad inspirational content

---

## Authority Evaluation Questions

- Does the page demonstrate engineering depth?
- Does it explain real technical problems?
- Does it support decision-making?
- Does it strengthen technical credibility?
- Does it differentiate MORFRAC?

---

# TASK PATTERN — CONTENT OPPORTUNITY ANALYSIS

## Objective

Identify commercially valuable SEO content opportunities.

Content must:
- support discoverability
- support authority
- support conversion pathways
- support technical differentiation

---

## High-Value Content Types

Preferred:
- technical explainers
- product decision pages
- integration guides
- engineering comparisons
- system architecture discussions
- application-specific pages
- failure-mode analysis

Low value:
- broad sailing news
- untargeted blog volume
- trend-chasing content
- low-intent informational content

---

## Opportunity Scoring

Prioritize opportunities with:
- existing impressions
- existing rankings
- commercial intent
- technical depth potential
- authority value
- strong product alignment

---

# TASK PATTERN — TRAFFIC QUALITY ANALYSIS

## Objective

Interpret traffic quality rather than traffic volume.

---

## Important Distinctions

High-value traffic:
- commercially relevant geographies
- technical-intent traffic
- engineering-related searches
- product discovery traffic

Potential noise:
- crawler/datacenter regions
- weak-intent traffic
- irrelevant discovery
- system-page traffic

---

## Evaluation Questions

- Is traffic commercially relevant?
- Is discovery scalable?
- Is traffic branded or non-branded?
- Does traffic align with technical positioning?
- Does traffic support lead generation?

---

# TASK PATTERN — EXECUTIVE SEO REPORTING

## Objective

Generate executive-level SEO intelligence.

Reports must answer:
- what matters
- what changed
- where leverage exists
- what should be prioritized
- what should be ignored
- what impacts discoverability
- what impacts authority

---

## Reporting Rules

Do:
- prioritize leverage
- connect findings to business impact
- distinguish noise from opportunity
- remain evidence-based

Do not:
- generate generic SEO summaries
- recommend broad untargeted actions
- overreact to short-term volatility
- focus on vanity metrics

---

# GLOBAL PRIORITY LOGIC

Always prioritize:

1. Commercial relevance
2. Technical authority
3. Discoverability
4. CTR improvement
5. Product visibility
6. Search intent alignment
7. Structural clarity

Never prioritize:

- traffic volume alone
- low-value impressions
- archive pages
- legal/system pages
- untargeted blogging
- generic SEO checklists

---

# SUCCESS CONDITIONS

The SEO system succeeds when:
- non-branded discoverability improves
- technical authority strengthens
- CTR improves
- product/category visibility improves
- commercial traffic quality improves
- search capture improves
- engineering differentiation becomes more visible
