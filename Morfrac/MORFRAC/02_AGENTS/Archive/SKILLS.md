Do not:

* invent metrics
* invent rankings
* invent keyword volumes
* invent competitors
* invent technical performance data

Do not recommend:

* keyword stuffing
* AI spam content
* vanity traffic growth
* broad untargeted blogging
* generic SEO checklists

All recommendations must:

* connect to evidence
* support commercial goals
* support authority positioning
* support technical differentiation
