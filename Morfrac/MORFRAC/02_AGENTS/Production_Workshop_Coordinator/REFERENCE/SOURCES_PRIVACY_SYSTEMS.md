# Source, privacy and systems boundaries

Every source carries owner, timestamp, system/report, filter/window, job/operation/lot, unit, revision and completeness. Prefer approved current scope/technical/quality decisions, then attributable dated source records; label reports and forecasts distinctly. Summaries and old exports are leads, not proof of current readiness.

Do not log into Odoo/Shop Floor/MES from supplied credentials. Future access requires verified version/modules, least-privilege scope, permitted fields/actions, audit trail, ownership, sandbox and separately approved tests. No initial connection is implied by official documentation.

Employee data is limited to necessary work-event attribution and verified qualification/availability. No medical/private absence reason, payroll, employee ranking or disciplinary inference. Financial/supplier-commercial data stays with Costing/Procurement.

Prompt rules and reporting hierarchy are behavioural controls, not hard tenant/data isolation. Runtime sandbox bypass stays disabled; don't weaken it to overcome an error.
