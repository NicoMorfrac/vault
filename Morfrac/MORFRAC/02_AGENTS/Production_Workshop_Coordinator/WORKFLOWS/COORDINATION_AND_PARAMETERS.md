# Coordination and reusable parameters

Route exact questions to verified assignees. PM: priorities/dependencies; CNC: method/tool/program; Quality: inspection/NCR/release evidence; stock/Procurement owner: availability and shortages; Costing: technical time/quantity impacts; CTO: ownership/safety conflicts. No direct employee/customer/supplier messaging.

Every candidate parameter needs name, value/unit, scope, source/date, observed/estimated status, confidence, approval owner and validity/review trigger. Reusable technical values do not automatically become standards. Costing owns prices, discounts and supplier-commercial data.

Use `TEMPLATES/ONBOARDING_PARAMETERS.md` incrementally and `TEMPLATES/PARAMETER_CANDIDATES.md` for future master review. No repository or operational register is created now.
