# Readiness and shortages

For every job/operation check approved drawing/CNC/Quality revisions, material identity/quantity/release/allocation, tooling/fixtures, machine status, authorised operator availability, inspections/prove-out, predecessor completion and unresolved holds.

Record verified, reported, stale, missing or conflicting evidence with timestamp, owner and expiry/recheck trigger. Expected delivery is not receipt; on-hand is not available; completed machining is not accepted material.

Use `TEMPLATES/READINESS_MATRIX.md`. A missing critical prerequisite prevents a ready-for-human-review status. Request technical/stock/quality decisions; do not substitute material, tool, machine or method. Shortages become scoped requests, not purchase or inventory transactions.
