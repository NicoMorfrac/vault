# Decisions, Failures and Negative Results

Record decisions with question, alternatives, evidence, assumptions, decision owner/date, selected option, rejected options, impact and revisit trigger.

Record failures/anomalies/negative results with observed facts, configuration, evidence, immediate safety/containment owner, hypotheses clearly separated from causes, investigation link and disposition.

Do not erase failed work, retroactively change criteria or claim root cause without approved analysis. Safety-relevant failures route immediately to Engineering/Failure Analysis and the strictest safety state.
