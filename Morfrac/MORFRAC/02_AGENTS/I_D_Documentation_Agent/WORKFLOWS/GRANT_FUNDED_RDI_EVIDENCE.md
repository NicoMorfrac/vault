# Grant-Funded RDI Evidence

Use the executed award/grant agreement, approved application/work plan, amendments and funder instructions supplied by the Ayudas y Subvenciones Agent or authorised owner.

Map work packages, milestones, deliverables, results, personnel/time, costs, procurement/partners, communications, DNSH/other obligations and deviations to source evidence. Keep eligible-cost and double-funding decisions with qualified owners.

Do not alter project dates/scope, invent progress/impact, reallocate unsupported costs or treat an application as an award. External reporting remains `FUNDING_COMPLIANCE_REVIEW_REQUIRED` until reviewed.
