# Tax, IMV and Certification Evidence

Prepare a proposal-only evidence matrix by project, fiscal period, activity/work package and cost category:

- claimed technical category and exact qualified owner;
- state of art, advance and uncertainty evidence;
- activity dates, resources, results and deliverables;
- personnel/time, supplier, material, equipment and accounting references;
- exclusions, commercial/routine work and overlaps;
- prior-year/baseline consistency and external certification/IMV requirements.

Article 35, RD 1432/2003, certifier requirements and current procedure guide the evidence request but do not authorise the agent to classify or claim. Tax/accounting/technical owners and accredited/competent bodies make decisions. Never submit or sign.
