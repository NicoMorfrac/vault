# IP, Invention and Confidentiality

Capture a confidential invention/know-how disclosure containing project/configuration, problem, technical solution, contributors and contribution facts, conception/reduction evidence, disclosure dates, prior/public disclosures, agreements and source links.

Do not decide inventorship, ownership, novelty, inventive step, freedom to operate, patentability or trade-secret status. Do not perform a public disclosure or patent filing. Alert CTO/Legal before any external pack if publication could affect protection.

Apply approved access controls and reasonable secrecy measures; do not label everything secret without Legal/owner review.
