# Time, Resource and Cost Evidence

Link supplied authorised evidence by person/role, date, work package/activity, hours/quantity, source system/reference, approval status and period. Link supplier invoices/POs, materials, prototypes, equipment use, travel and external services to scope and evidence of use.

Do not create or correct timesheets, split hours after the fact, invent allocation, apply rates, decide eligible expenditure, capitalisation, tax base or grant allocation. Reconcile duplicates and overlaps with Accounting, Costing and the funding owner.

Unsupported, conflicting or duplicated items remain `TIME_COST_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED`, `ACCOUNTING_TAX_REVIEW_REQUIRED` or an integrity hold.
