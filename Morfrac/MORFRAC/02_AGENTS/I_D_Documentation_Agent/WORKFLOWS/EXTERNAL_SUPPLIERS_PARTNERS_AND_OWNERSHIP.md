# External Suppliers, Partners and Ownership

Record entity, role, work package, deliverable, background/foreground inputs, source agreement/PO, confidentiality, IP/data ownership, publication rights, personnel, cost evidence and acceptance status.

Do not infer ownership or licence rights from payment, file possession or authorship. Do not contact or commit a supplier/partner. Route contract, IP, data and publication questions to Legal and technical acceptance to Engineering.

Missing terms/commitment/evidence remains `PARTNER_OWNERSHIP_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.
