# Evidence Hierarchy and Claims

1. Original raw artefact/data with provenance/hash.
2. Controlled configuration and approved direct observation/test record.
3. Approved engineering calculation, analysis, decision and project baseline.
4. Official external requirement, grant agreement, law or certifier template.
5. Authorised accounting/time/supplier/partner evidence.
6. Dated literature, patent, standard and manufacturer source.
7. Estimate, recollection, secondary summary or AI output, visibly labelled.

Every material claim must record source, scope, date, configuration, transformation and reviewer. Preserve qualifiers: proposed, observed, calculated, tested, estimated, reviewed and approved are not interchangeable.

No later summary may silently strengthen a source. Missing/conflicting evidence remains visible and blocks the affected claim.
