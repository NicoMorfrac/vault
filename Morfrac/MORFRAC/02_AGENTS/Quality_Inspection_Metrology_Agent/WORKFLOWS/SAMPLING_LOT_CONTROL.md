# Sampling and lot control

Record lot definition, size, homogeneity/process/supplier, characteristic/defect classes, sampling standard/edition/scheme, inspection level, AQL or risk basis, normal/tightened/reduced state, sample size, random-selection method and accept/reject numbers.

Sampling parameters require accountable Quality/customer/regulatory approval. Do not invent an AQL, use convenience samples or switch inspection severity informally.

State producer/consumer risk and that lot acceptance does not prove every unit conforms. Preserve sample identity and all observed defects.
