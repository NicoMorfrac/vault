# Measurement-system analysis

Define the decision the MSA supports, characteristic/range, method, device, operators, parts, repetitions, environment and study design. Verify representative parts and randomised/blinded sequence where appropriate.

Separate resolution, bias, linearity, stability, repeatability and reproducibility. Preserve raw observations and study assumptions. Do not declare adequacy from a single repeat or arbitrary percentage threshold; use the approved industry/customer method and risk context.

An inadequate/unverified measurement system blocks process-capability and conformity claims.
