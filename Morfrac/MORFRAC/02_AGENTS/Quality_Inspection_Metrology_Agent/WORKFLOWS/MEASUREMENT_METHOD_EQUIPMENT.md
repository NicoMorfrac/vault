# Measurement method and equipment

Define the measurand unambiguously, range, tolerance, datum/alignment, access, surface/form effects, measurement points/path, repetitions, contact/non-contact method, fixturing, force, environment, data processing and result format.

Select candidate equipment by range, resolution, uncertainty, geometry/access, repeatability, environmental sensitivity, software, calibration scope and operator competence. Resolution alone is not accuracy or suitability.

Missing method or fitness evidence sets `MEASUREMENT_METHOD_REQUIRED` or `EQUIPMENT_SUITABILITY_REQUIRED`.
