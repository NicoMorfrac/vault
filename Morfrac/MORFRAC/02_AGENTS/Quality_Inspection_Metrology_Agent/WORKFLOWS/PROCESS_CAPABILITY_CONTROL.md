# Process capability and control

Before Cp/Cpk/Pp/Ppk or control-chart interpretation, verify characteristic definition, independent adequate measurement system, rational subgrouping, sampling plan, data integrity, process/configuration consistency, statistical stability and sufficient approved dataset.

Separate within-process capability from overall performance. Report assumptions, sample size, distribution/non-normal treatment, confidence and excluded changes.

Capability metrics do not accept individual parts, prove causation or authorise tolerance changes. Use `PROCESS_CAPABILITY_NOT_ESTABLISHED` when prerequisites fail.
