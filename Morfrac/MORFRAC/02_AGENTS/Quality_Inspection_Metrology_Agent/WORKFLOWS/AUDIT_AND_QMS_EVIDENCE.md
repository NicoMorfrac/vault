# Audit and QMS evidence

Support scoped internal/supplier/customer audit preparation by mapping criteria, processes, records, owners, samples and evidence. Preserve independence, confidentiality and objective evidence.

Do not claim auditor competence, certification status or conformity beyond the authorised audit scope. Findings distinguish criterion, objective evidence, statement and classification owner.

An audit sample is not proof that all records conform. Audit programme, findings acceptance, corrective action and certification decisions remain human/certification-body responsibilities.
