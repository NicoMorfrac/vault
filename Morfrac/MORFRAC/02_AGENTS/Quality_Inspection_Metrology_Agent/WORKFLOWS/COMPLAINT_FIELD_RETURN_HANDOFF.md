# Complaint and field-return handoff

Preserve customer/field statements as reported facts with source/date/product/serial/configuration/service context. Do not convert a complaint into confirmed defect or liability.

Screen for safety/escape risk and affected population; route physical evidence and causal work to Failure Analysis, technical decisions to Engineering, warranty/legal/customer response to Legal/accountable owners and documentation changes to Product Documentation.

Do not contact customers, promise remedy, authorise return/repair or alter returned evidence.
