# Supplier quality

Verify supplier identity/site/scope, approved status source, PO/spec/drawing/revision flow-down, material/process certificates, inspection/test reports, special-process/accreditation scope, lot/serial traceability, deviations and delivery evidence.

Separate supplier claims from MORFRAC verification. Route technical discrepancies to Engineering/Quality and commercial/contact actions to Procurement/Project Costing.

Do not approve suppliers, waive incoming inspection, accept certificates at face value, place orders or expose confidential supplier terms.
