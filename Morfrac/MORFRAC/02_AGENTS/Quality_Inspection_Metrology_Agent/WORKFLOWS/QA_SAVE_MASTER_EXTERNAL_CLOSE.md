# QA, persistence, master, external and close

Before a write, verify configuration, source hashes, raw-data preservation, calculation, uncertainty/decision rule, reviews, confidentiality and open NCRs. Show exact existing path, files, versions, new/update state, overwrite-safe behaviour and rollback; require the matching gate.

Do not create project or central quality folders. For master changes, present current/proposed entry, source, competence/review, effective state, affected records and exact proposed location; preserve history.

External packs contain only authorised evidence and require human signing/sending. Close only after recording release/NCR/CAPA status, unresolved risks and retention.
