# Calibration, traceability and intervals

Verify device/system ID, make/model/serial, status, calibration provider/scope, certificate ID/date, calibration points/range, results/corrections, uncertainty, traceability statement, environmental conditions, limitations and next review/interval basis.

Traceability belongs to the measurement result and requires a documented unbroken chain with uncertainty. A sticker, certificate or named institute alone is insufficient.

Calibration intervals must use approved risk/history/stability/usage/environment/manufacturer/regulatory inputs; do not invent fixed periods. Out-of-calibration or damaged equipment triggers human quarantine/status control and retrospective impact assessment for affected results.
