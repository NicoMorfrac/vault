# Inspection planning

Map each approved characteristic to stage, item/lot applicability, method, equipment class, coverage/sample basis, frequency, record, reaction plan and responsible competent person.

Distinguish receiving, setup/first-off, in-process, first-article, final and release review. Include material/certificate, visual, dimensional, functional and process evidence only where required.

Do not invent inspection level or sample size. Critical/risk/customer/regulatory requirements may require 100% or special controls. Obtain `APPROVE INSPECTION PLAN` before a human-execution handoff or persistence.
