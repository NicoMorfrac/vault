# Official source baseline

Verified 2026-08-30. Confirm licensed text, contractual adoption, jurisdiction and current edition before use.

## Quality and measurement management

- ISO 9001:2015/Amd 1:2024 remains the published QMS requirements edition on this date: https://www.iso.org/standard/62085.html
- ISO's sixth edition is under publication and expected September 2026; it is not assumed effective: https://www.iso.org/standard/88464.html
- ISO 10012:2026 specifies measurement-management-system requirements and replaces ISO 10012:2003: https://www.iso.org/standard/10012
- ISO/IEC 17025:2017 addresses competence, impartiality and consistent operation of testing/calibration laboratories: https://www.iso.org/standard/66912.html
- ISO 19011:2026 provides management-system audit guidance: https://www.iso.org/standard/19011

## Metrology, uncertainty and decision rules

- BIPM/JCGM guides, including JCGM 100:2008 and current amendments: https://www.bipm.org/en/web/guest/publications/guides
- JCGM 200:2012 International Vocabulary of Metrology: https://www.bipm.org/en/doi/10.59161/jcgm200-2012
- NIST metrological traceability policy and FAQ: https://www.nist.gov/metrology/metrological-traceability
- ISO/IEC Guide 98-1:2024 uncertainty introduction: https://www.iso.org/standard/82708.html
- ISO 14253-1:2017 GPS conformity decision rules, current/confirmed in 2023: https://www.iso.org/standard/70137.html
- ILAC G8:09/2019 decision-rule and conformity-statement guidance: https://ilac.org/latest_ilac_news/revised-ilac-g8-published/
- ILAC P10:07/2020 metrological-traceability policy: https://ilac.org/latest_ilac_news/revised-ilac-p10-published/
- ILAC G24:2022 recalibration-interval guidance: https://ilac.org/latest_ilac_news/revised-ilac-g24-document-published/

## Sampling

- ISO 2859-1:2026 is the current lot-by-lot attribute-sampling edition and replaces the 1999 edition: https://www.iso.org/standard/85464.html
