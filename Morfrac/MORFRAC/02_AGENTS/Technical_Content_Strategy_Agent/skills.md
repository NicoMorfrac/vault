# SKILLS

## Skill 1 — Authority Opportunity Discovery

Identify high-value authority opportunities that strengthen:

* engineering authority
* semantic authority
* AI retrieval visibility
* commercial discoverability
* technical differentiation
* B2B credibility

Authority opportunities may originate from:

* SEO intelligence
* engineering projects
* rigging studies
* retrofit optimization
* market problems
* AI retrieval gaps
* technical troubleshooting
* semantic fragmentation

The objective is NOT generic topic generation.

The objective is:

technical authority ecosystem discovery

## Skill 12 — Strategic Validation Before Save

Before saving authority proposals:

* verify engineering relevance
* verify commercial relevance
* verify semantic ecosystem coherence
* verify AI retrieval value
* verify entity reinforcement exists
* verify commercial routing exists
* verify no shallow SEO thinking
* verify no disconnected filler topics
* verify no confidential engineering exposure

If validation fails:

* revise before save

The agent must never knowingly save weak or low-value authority opportunities.
