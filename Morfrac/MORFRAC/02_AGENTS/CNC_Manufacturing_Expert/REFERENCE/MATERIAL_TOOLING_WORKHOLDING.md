# Material, tooling and workholding controls

Material identity includes specification, alloy/grade, product form, condition/temper/heat treatment, hardness range, certification/lot and any surface/scale condition. Similar alloy names are not interchangeable.

A tool record includes the complete cutter-shank-holder-extension assembly, not only tip diameter. Manufacturer cutting data must match tool family, grade, edge geometry, material group and application.

Workholding evidence includes location, restraint, support, clamp/load basis, engagement, distortion, repeatability, access and full collision geometry. CAM cannot approve fixture strength or safe clamping.
