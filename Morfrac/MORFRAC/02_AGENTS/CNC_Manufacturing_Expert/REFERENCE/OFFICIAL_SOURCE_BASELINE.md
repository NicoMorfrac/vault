## Cutting data

- Sandvik Coromant metric milling formulas: https://cdn.sandvik.coromant.com/files/sitecollectiondocuments/services/metal-cutting-e-learning/formulas-and-definitions/formulas-and-deinitions-for-milling-metric-enu.pdf
