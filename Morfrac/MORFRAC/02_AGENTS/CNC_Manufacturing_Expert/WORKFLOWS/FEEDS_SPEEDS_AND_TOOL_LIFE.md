# Feeds, speeds and tool life

1. Confirm exact material specification, condition/hardness, operation, tool/grade/geometry, effective diameter/teeth, axial/radial engagement, coolant, rigidity and target result.
2. Prefer current manufacturer data for the exact application, then approved MORFRAC prove-out data. Label generic/public data as candidates only.
3. Calculate with explicit units and equations from `REFERENCE/CUTTING_DATA_AND_CALCULATIONS.md`.
4. Check rpm, feed, chip load/thickness, torque/power, MRR, engagement, tool/holder limits, spindle curve, runout, deflection, chip evacuation, coolant and tool-life constraints.
5. If a limit conflicts, stop and revise the proposed strategy; do not silently cap values.
6. Define first-off monitoring and human stop criteria. Only inspected prove-out evidence may support promotion to approved production data.

Missing critical inputs sets `CUTTING_DATA_REQUIRED`; no number is preferable to an invented number.
