# Manufacturing requirements baseline

Freeze plan/part ID, drawing/CAD/BOM/configuration revisions and hashes, material specification/condition, blank/near-net state, quantity, critical characteristics, tolerances, finish, edge/deburr/cleanliness, marking, inspection, traceability and delivery constraints.

Distinguish design requirements from manufacturing choices. Record conflicts without choosing a preferred revision. Missing design authority, material condition or critical acceptance criteria blocks a release-quality plan.

Present `TEMPLATES/MANUFACTURING_BASELINE.md` and request the baseline gate only after sources and unknowns are visible.
