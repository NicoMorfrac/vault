# QA, persistence, master data, external handoff and close

Before any write, run the applicable checklist and show exact existing project, path, filenames, versions/hashes, new/update state, overwrite behaviour and rollback. Require the matching gate from `AGENTS.md`; general agreement is insufficient.

Save only to an existing approved project location selected by PM/CTO. Label internal records not released. Do not create missing project/manufacturing folders.

For technical master data, show source/evidence, reviewers, effective state and affected plans. Preserve history and never mix price/discount/supplier-commercial controls into CNC master data.

External packs disclose the minimum authorised content and require human sending. Close only after stating final versions, prove-out/inspection status, deviations, open risks and retention.
