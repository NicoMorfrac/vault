* prioritization

# TASK\_PATTERN\_01\_STRATEGIC\_OPPORTUNITY\_EVALUATION

# OBJECTIVE

Evaluate whether a detected opportunity is strategically valuable for MORFRAC.

***

# INPUTS

* market intelligence
* B2B problem findings
* competitor weaknesses
* commercial signals
* supplier intelligence
* engineering constraints

***

# PROCESS

## STEP 1 — DEFINE THE OPPORTUNITY

Clearly identify:

* what problem exists
* who experiences the problem
* why the problem matters
* how the problem is currently addressed

Avoid vague opportunity definitions.

***

## STEP 2 — IDENTIFY THE TARGET SEGMENT

Classify:

* industry segment
* customer type
* technical profile
* operational profile

Determine:

* who would realistically pay
* who experiences the pain most strongly

***

## STEP 3 — EVALUATE STRATEGIC ALIGNMENT

Evaluate alignment with:

* MORFRAC engineering capabilities
* manufacturing capabilities
* integration capabilities
* retrofit expertise
* customization capability
* operational structure

Assign:

* MORFRAC\_FIT\_SCORE

***

## STEP 4 — EVALUATE COMMERCIAL POTENTIAL

Estimate:

* willingness to pay
* repeatability
* scalability
* margin potential
* support burden
* implementation cost

Assign:

* COMMERCIAL\_POTENTIAL\_SCORE

***

## STEP 5 — EVALUATE COMPETITIVE POSITION

Analyze:

* existing competitors
* competitor weaknesses
* market saturation
* differentiation potential
* technical defensibility

Determine:

* whether MORFRAC has a meaningful advantage

***

## STEP 6 — EVALUATE RISKS

Identify:

* operational risks
* engineering risks
* manufacturing risks
* scaling risks
* support burden
* dependency risks

Never ignore operational complexity.

***

## STEP 7 — DETERMINE STRATEGIC VALUE

Determine whether the opportunity:

* strengthens long-term positioning
* increases technical leverage
* improves defensibility
* creates recurring B2B relationships
* increases engineering value capture

***

# REQUIRED OUTPUT

* Opportunity Summary
* Industry Segment
* Problem Being Solved
* Strategic Importance
* Commercial Potential
* Competitive Landscape
* MORFRAC Strategic Advantage
* Risks
* Confidence Level
* Recommended Actions

* DISCOVERY
* VALIDATION\_REQUIRED
* VALIDATING
* STRATEGIC\_OPPORTUNITY
* COMMERCIAL\_OPPORTUNITY
* DEFERRED
* REJECTED

Provide rationale.

Status progression should be evidence-based.

Example:

Opportunity:
Retrofit Validation Package

Status:
VALIDATION\_REQUIRED
