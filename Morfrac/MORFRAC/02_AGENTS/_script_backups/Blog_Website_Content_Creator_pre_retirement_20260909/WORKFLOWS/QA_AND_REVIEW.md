# Workflow - QA and Review

## Required checks

### Brief compliance

- Requested asset, audience, objective, language, deliverables, and CTA are satisfied.
- Upstream SEO requirements and approved commercial routing are preserved.

### Factual integrity

- Every material claim is confirmed, cited in review notes, explicitly marked for verification, or removed.
- No invented MORFRAC specifications, outcomes, certifications, customers, comparisons, prices, or guarantees appear.
- Time-sensitive information includes a source date.

### Confidentiality and rights

- No client identity, protected project data, proprietary calculation, credential, personal data, or unreleased detail is exposed.
- Copy is original and does not closely imitate a source or competitor.

### Content quality

- The opening answers a real reader need.
- Headings form a coherent information hierarchy.
- Paragraphs add information; filler and repetition are removed.
- Tone matches MORFRAC's engineering-led voice.
- Claims and CTA match the funnel stage.

### Search and AI retrieval

- Search intent is satisfied without keyword stuffing.
- Definitions, entities, relationships, trade-offs, and answer sections are explicit where useful.
- Internal-link suggestions are approved or labelled proposed.
- No ranking, indexing, traffic, or AI-inclusion guarantee is made.

### Channel fit

- Blog and website content are distinct from social derivatives.
- SEO metadata is not confused with Meta-platform content.
- Platform adaptations preserve the verified master claim set.

## Outcomes

- Pass: `DRAFT_READY` or proceed to `SAVE_PENDING_APPROVAL`.
- Material fact missing: `QA_BLOCKED` with exact claim and required owner/evidence.
- Brief conflict: `NEEDS_INPUT`.

Never hide a failed check by weakening the wording without reporting the unresolved issue.
