# Incremental workshop onboarding

Collect only what the current task needs; leave unknowns explicit.

1. Production owner and PM priority source.
2. Work-center/machine IDs and approved capability sources (CNC owns technical details).
3. Working calendars, downtime/maintenance and simultaneous capacity, with timezone and owner.
4. Authorised operator availability and required qualifications, without wages or private absence reasons.
5. Material/lot availability, release and allocations; source owner and freshness.
6. Tools/fixtures/inspection resources and confirmation owner.
7. Setup/run/cleanup/wait/inspection time definitions and observed evidence.
8. Job/operation/lot identifiers, permitted status transitions and original-record sources.
9. Reporting recipients, reconciliation cadence on demand and approval owners.
10. Proposed future storage/system connection for a separate approval task.
