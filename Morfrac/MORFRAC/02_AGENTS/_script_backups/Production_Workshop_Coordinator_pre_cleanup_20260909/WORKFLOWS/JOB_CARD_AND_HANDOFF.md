# Job card and handoff

Compile references to approved technical and quality instructions rather than rewriting machining or safety procedures. Bind exact job/operation/lot/configuration, machine/tool/fixture identifiers, quantities, prerequisites, work instructions and mandatory hold points.

Mark the draft `FOR HUMAN REVIEW - NOT A PRODUCTION RELEASE`. Missing released work instructions or superseded revisions blocks handoff readiness. Record CNC/Quality and human production approvals separately.

After the exact workshop handoff gate, provide the scoped pack only through authorised Paperclip coordination. It does not start work, assign a worker, release NC, override a hold or authorise rework. See `TEMPLATES/JOB_CARD.md`.
