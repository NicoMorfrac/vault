# Holds, changes and recovery

A drawing, program, material, fixture, process, machine, operator-qualification, inspection or quantity change triggers an impact review for affected cards, reservations, approvals and downstream work. Do not relabel the old revision as current.

Preserve CNC/Quality/Engineering holds with issuer, scope, reason and required lifting evidence. Only the accountable owner can resolve the hold. Request human confirmation of physical containment; do not claim it occurred.

Propose recovery options within existing authority and expose PM date/capacity/cost impact. Overtime, subcontracting, substitution, rework, scrap and release need their respective owners. Record integrity/safety concerns immediately using the lead holds in `AGENTS.md`.
