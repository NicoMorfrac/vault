# Official Source Baseline

Baseline checked: 2026-08-30. This is a research starting point, not a product-scope conclusion. Before use, verify current consolidation, effective/applicable dates, territory, economic-operator role, transitional provisions, national implementation and exact product configuration with the accountable compliance/Legal owner.

## European Union

- General Product Safety Regulation (EU) 2023/988: applies from 2024-12-13; consider only where the product and activity are in scope. Official text: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj
- European Commission GPSR business guidance, Commission Notice C/2025/6233: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/C/2025/6233/oj
- European Commission Safety Business Gateway guidance, Commission Notice C/2025/6238: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/C/2025/6238/oj
- Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230: generally applies from 2027-01-20, subject to its provisions and transition. Official text: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1230/oj
- Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC: remains relevant under the transition through 2027-01-19 where applicable. Official text: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2006/42/oj
- Recreational Craft Directive 2013/53/EU: screen exact product/component and activity; marine use alone does not establish scope. Official text: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2013/53/oj
- Market Surveillance Regulation (EU) 2019/1020: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2019/1020/oj
- Accreditation and CE-marking framework Regulation (EC) 765/2008: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2008/765/oj
- Blue Guide on EU product rules (2022): https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:52022XC0629(04)
- Product Liability Directive (EU) 2024/2853: Legal Agent must verify transition and Spanish transposition before reliance. Official text: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2024/2853/oj

## Spain

- Real Decreto 1644/2008 on machinery placement in the market/service: verify consolidated text and transition; instructions for Spain require the applicable Spanish-language treatment. https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2008-16387
- Real Decreto 98/2016 implementing recreational-craft requirements: screen actual product/component scope. https://www.boe.es/eli/es/rd/2016/03/11/98/con
- Real Decreto Legislativo 1/2007, consumer protection and guarantees: Legal Agent must determine consumer/B2B applicability, current text and warranty wording. https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2007-20555

## Standards and third-party sources

- Use only the exact approved standard/specification edition named by the accountable compliance/Engineering owner.
- Record amendment, national adoption, withdrawal/transition and product/configuration applicability.
- Do not reproduce licensed standard content unless MORFRAC has the necessary rights.
- Supplier declarations and instructions are evidence inputs, not proof of MORFRAC product conformity without reconciliation.
