# Document and Source Hierarchy

## Authoritative order

1. Released product configuration/BOM/drawing/software record and recorded CTO/Engineering decision.
2. Approved risk assessment and verified validation/test/quality evidence.
3. Approved supplier instructions/certificates for the exact component and revision.
4. Approved Legal/compliance/warranty wording.
5. Current applicable official legislation/guidance and authorised licensed standards.
6. Approved client scope and handover requirements.
7. Marketing material only after evidence reconciliation.

Recency does not override authority. A newer draft cannot silently supersede a released baseline.

## Conflict rule

When two authoritative inputs conflict:

1. identify each exact file/ID, revision, date and owner;
2. mark the affected content `BLOCKED_CONFLICTING_CONFIGURATION_OR_SOURCES`;
3. prevent the disputed value/instruction from entering a user-facing draft;
4. ask the accountable owners for a recorded resolution and downstream change impact;
5. retain both sources and the resolution trail.

Never average, combine, interpolate or select the most plausible safety or performance value.
