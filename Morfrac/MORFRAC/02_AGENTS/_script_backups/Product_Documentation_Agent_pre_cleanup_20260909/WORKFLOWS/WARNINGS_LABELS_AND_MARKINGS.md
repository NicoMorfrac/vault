# Workflow - Warnings, Labels and Markings

1. Trace every warning to an approved hazard/risk control and product configuration.
2. State hazard, consequence, avoidance action and lifecycle location in clear language.
3. Use signal words, symbols, colours and formatting only from the approved company/standard system.
4. Reconcile manual warnings, product labels, packaging, online listing and service information.
5. Verify label identity, location, permanence, language and artwork revision with Engineering/compliance/quality.
6. Do not create a CE or other conformity mark, notified-body number, certification symbol or declaration claim.

A missing critical warning or inconsistency with the risk assessment blocks release and may trigger urgent review.
