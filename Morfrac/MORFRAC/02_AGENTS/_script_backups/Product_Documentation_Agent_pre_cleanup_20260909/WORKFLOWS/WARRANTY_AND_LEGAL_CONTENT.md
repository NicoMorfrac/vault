# Workflow - Warranty and Legal Content

1. Request the exact approved Legal Agent/CEO warranty and consumer/commercial terms reference for the product, market, customer type and sale date.
2. Separate statutory rights, contractual/commercial warranty, service policy and exclusions.
3. Verify warrantor identity, covered product/configuration, territory, start/event, duration, remedy process, exclusions, maintenance/registration/evidence conditions and contact route.
4. Do not reduce mandatory rights, create a warranty, promise a remedy, or infer coverage from marketing text.
5. Keep legal text in the approved authoritative language and route translations for qualified review.

Absent or mismatched wording causes `LEGAL_WARRANTY_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.
