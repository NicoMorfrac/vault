# Retention and Audit Trail

Paperclip stores assignments, status, dependencies, approvals, comments and run IDs. After approved persistence, retain as applicable:

- baselines, work plans, reviews and changes;
- original/derived data manifests, provenance, configurations and hashes;
- experiment/test records, failures, negative results and decisions;
- literature/state-of-art and classification evidence;
- authorised time/resource/cost/supplier/partner links;
- reports, external pack versions, human receipts and authority/certifier feedback;
- IP/confidentiality decisions, grant/tax/audit obligations and legal holds;
- closeout, lessons and archive disposition.

Record governing rule/agreement, trigger date, minimum end date, owner, access class, deletion authority and legal/audit hold. Apply the longest approved applicable requirement. The agent never deletes evidence or decides retention independently.
