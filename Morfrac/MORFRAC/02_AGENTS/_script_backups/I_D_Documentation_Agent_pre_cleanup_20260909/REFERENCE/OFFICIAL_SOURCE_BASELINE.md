## Spanish R&D and technological-innovation tax evidence

- Ley 27/2014 del Impuesto sobre Sociedades, Article 35 and related provisions: https://www.boe.es/eli/es/l/2014/11/27/27/con
- Real Decreto 1432/2003 on reasoned reports for scientific/technological requirements: https://www.boe.es/eli/es/rd/2003/11/21/1432/con
- Ministry information and current documents for Informes Motivados Vinculantes: https://www.ciencia.gob.es/Innovar/Informes-Motivados-Vinculantes.html
- ENAC I+D+i accreditation information and current accredited-certifier scope: https://www.enac.es/que-hacemos/sectores/i-d-i

## Accounting

- Real Decreto 1514/2007, Plan General de Contabilidad, including current intangible/R&D recognition rules: https://www.boe.es/eli/es/rd/2007/11/16/1514/con

Accounting treatment and tax eligibility require qualified current advice; documentation alone does not establish them.

## Funded R&D

- CDTI R&D project information and project-specific conditions: https://www.cdti.es/ayudas/proyectos-de-i-d
- European Commission guidance for managing grants, technical/financial reporting, deliverables, audits and record keeping: https://commission.europa.eu/funding-and-tenders/managing-your-project/managing-your-project-under-grant-agreement_en

The executed award/grant agreement, approved work plan, amendments and current programme instructions control each funded project.
