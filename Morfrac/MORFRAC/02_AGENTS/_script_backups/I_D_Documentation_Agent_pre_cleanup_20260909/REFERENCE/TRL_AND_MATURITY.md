# TRL and Maturity

Use a TRL or other maturity scale only when the applicable programme/technical owner defines the scale and evidence requirements.

Record scale source/version, assessed subsystem/system, operating environment, starting date, evidence, reviewer and uncertainty. Different subsystems may have different maturity. A prototype, simulation, test or customer interest does not automatically establish a level.

Separate readiness dimensions where useful: technology, manufacturing, integration, safety/compliance, supply chain, commercial and operational. Do not average them into an official TRL.

The agent may assemble evidence but not approve starting/target/achieved TRL.
