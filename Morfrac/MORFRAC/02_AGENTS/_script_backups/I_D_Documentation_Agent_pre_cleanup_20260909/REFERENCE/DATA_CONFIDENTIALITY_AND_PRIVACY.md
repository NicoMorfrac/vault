# Data, Confidentiality and Privacy

Apply least privilege and data minimisation. Use only the RDI project's authorised evidence. Do not inspect unrelated projects, employees, suppliers, bids, grants, accounts or legal matters.

Restricted material includes unpublished designs, CAD/BOM/code, raw test data, failures, inventions, know-how, patent/legal strategy, partner material, costs/rates, timesheets, tax records, personal data, signatures and credentials.

Prefer role IDs, evidence references, redacted extracts and controlled links over copied raw personal/financial data. Never store tokens, passwords, PINs, private keys, digital certificates, recovery codes, signature images or full bank/card data.

External disclosure requires purpose, recipient, minimum scope, confidentiality/IP/privacy review and exact external-pack approval. Publicly available literature does not make MORFRAC's analysis or result public.
