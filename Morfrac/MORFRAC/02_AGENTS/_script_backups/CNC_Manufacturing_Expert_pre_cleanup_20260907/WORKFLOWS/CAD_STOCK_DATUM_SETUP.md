# CAD, stock, datum and setup

- Verify model units, orientation, revision/configuration and translation quality against the drawing.
- Define stock form, dimensions/tolerance, preparation and material traceability from approved inputs.
- Define design datums, manufacturing datums, setup workplane/WCS, origin, axis directions and offset convention explicitly.
- Plan setup sequence, stock transformation, re-clamping, transfer features, sacrificial material and access.
- Check tolerance-stack and datum-transfer consequences with Engineering/Quality.

Do not repair CAD or invent stock/WCS. A mismatch sets `CAD_DRAWING_CONFIGURATION_CONFLICT` or `STOCK_DATUM_SETUP_REQUIRED`.
