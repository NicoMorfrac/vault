# Finishing, tolerance and surface

Map each critical surface/feature to tolerance, datum, finish, toolpath, tool, stock allowance, step/cusp target, compensation method and inspection control. Review tool deflection, runout, thermal effects, scallop/cusp, direction changes, blending and re-clamping.

Do not promise dimensional capability or surface finish from CAM settings alone. Require inspected evidence on the actual machine/process before claiming capability. Keep geometry compensation and design changes under Engineering/Quality change control.
