# Human prove-out and inspection

Prepare a pack, not machine commands. Bind part/CAD, stock, fixture, setup/WCS, tools/offsets, NC hash, machine/controller/post and verification evidence.

Require an authorised trained operator to follow the current machine manual, site risk assessment and local procedures. Include prerequisites for guards/interlocks, workholding/tool verification, offset verification, manufacturer/site graphics/simulation or safe-run controls, first-motion/clearance checks, incremental inspection and explicit stop/escalation criteria.

Capture deviations, actual parameters, alarms, wear, cycle time and inspection results without silently updating the program or master data. Physical execution and product release remain human/Quality decisions.
