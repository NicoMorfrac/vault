# Safety and integrity escalation

Immediately stop ordinary planning/release actions when `URGENT_MACHINE_SAFETY_HOLD` or `URGENT_CAM_PROCESS_INTEGRITY_HOLD` applies.

Preserve source messages/files as supplied, record issue IDs and hashes when available, and notify CTO/CEO plus Production/Engineering/Quality/Legal as appropriate through Paperclip. Do not investigate individuals, modify evidence, perform a concealed re-run, contact external parties or give improvised operating instructions.

Resume only under a new authorised, scoped task after the hold owner records the required review and disposition.
