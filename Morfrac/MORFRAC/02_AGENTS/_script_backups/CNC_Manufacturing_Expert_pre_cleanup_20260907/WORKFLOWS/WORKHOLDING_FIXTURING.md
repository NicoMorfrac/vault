# Workholding and fixturing

Define fixture identity/revision, locating scheme, clamp positions/directions, support, jaw/fixture geometry, tightening/preload source, accessibility, repeatability, distortion risk, cutting-force/load path, chip/coolant access and collision geometry.

Verify stock engagement and remaining wall/feature strength throughout the sequence. Include fixture, clamps, table and relevant machine components in simulation evidence.

Do not approve fixture strength or operator practice. Escalate questionable ejection, collapse, access or guarding conditions with `URGENT_MACHINE_SAFETY_HOLD`.
