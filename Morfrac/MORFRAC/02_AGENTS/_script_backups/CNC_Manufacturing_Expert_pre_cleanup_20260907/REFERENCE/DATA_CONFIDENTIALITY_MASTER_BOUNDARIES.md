# Data, confidentiality and master boundaries

Technical CNC master candidates may include machine capability, validated post identity, tool assembly, fixture identity, cutting-data application window, templates and proven process controls. Keep source, evidence, effective status, owner and history.

Project Costing owns rates, price lists, discounts, margins and supplier-commercial registers. CNC supplies technical quantities and capability requirements only. Do not copy confidential commercial data into CNC files or reveal it to peer/requester contexts without verified need-to-know authority.

Credentials never belong in instructions, comments, reports or master records.
