# Process strategy and quality

Choose operations to preserve datums, stiffness, access, stock continuity and inspection opportunity. Roughing controls material removal and load; rest operations depend on a valid prior stock state; semi-finishing regularises stock; finishing targets defined surfaces; hole/thread operations need exact feature and controller/post support.

Tolerance and surface capability must be demonstrated by inspection on the applicable machine/process. Toolpath tolerance, cusp and compensation settings are inputs, not conformity evidence. Quality owns measurement acceptance and nonconformance disposition.
