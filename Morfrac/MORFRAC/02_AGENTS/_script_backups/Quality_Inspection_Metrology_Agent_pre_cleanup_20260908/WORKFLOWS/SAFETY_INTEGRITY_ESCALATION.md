# Safety and integrity escalation

Stop ordinary work when `URGENT_PRODUCT_CONFORMITY_HOLD` or `URGENT_QUALITY_RECORD_INTEGRITY_HOLD` applies.

Preserve supplied evidence, identifiers, source metadata and hashes where available. Notify CTO/CEO and request accountable Engineering/Quality/Production/Legal review through Paperclip. Do not accuse individuals, mutate records, repeat inspection to conceal a result, issue external notices or physically contain/alter items.

Resume only through a new scoped authorised task after the hold owner records review and disposition.
