# Disposition, deviation and concession

Prepare options only after Engineering/Quality/Production and Legal/customer/regulatory implications are identified. Separate use-as-is, rework, repair, scrap and return-to-supplier; define verification and traceability needs for each.

The agent cannot choose or approve a disposition, deviation or concession. Require accountable signatures/authority and customer/regulator approval where applicable.

No tolerance broadening, undocumented blend/repair, serial substitution or retroactive approval is permitted. Set `HUMAN_NCR_DECISION_REQUIRED`.
