# Requirements and characteristic baseline

Freeze drawing/specification/BOM/CAD/configuration revisions/hashes and units. Extract each characteristic verbatim with identifier, nominal, limits/tolerance, datum/reference, criticality/classification, applicable item/lot and source.

Record conflicts, ambiguities and missing acceptance criteria without interpreting design intent. Engineering owns requirement interpretation and criticality. Quality owns inspection classification only after approval.

Use `CONFIGURATION_REVISION_CONFLICT`, `QUALITY_REQUIREMENTS_REQUIRED` or `CHARACTERISTIC_CLASSIFICATION_REQUIRED` until resolved.
