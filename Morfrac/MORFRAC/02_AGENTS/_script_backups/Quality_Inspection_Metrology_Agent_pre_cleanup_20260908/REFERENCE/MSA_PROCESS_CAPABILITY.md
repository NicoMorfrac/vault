# MSA and process capability

Measurement-system analysis evaluates whether the measurement process is adequate for its intended decisions. Applicable components may include resolution, bias, linearity, stability, repeatability and reproducibility.

Process capability/performance analysis additionally requires a consistent characteristic/process/configuration, adequate measurement system, suitable sampling, statistical stability and enough data. Cp/Cpk describe within-process capability under assumptions; Pp/Ppk describe overall performance under assumptions.

Metrics do not prove cause, accept individual units, justify tolerance changes or replace control plans.
