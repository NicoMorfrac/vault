# Sampling and lot risk

An acceptance-sampling plan requires a defined homogeneous lot, defect/characteristic classes, approved standard/edition/scheme, inspection level, AQL or other risk basis, switching status, sample size, accept/reject numbers and random selection.

AQL is not a promise that a stated percentage of the submitted lot is conforming, and lot acceptance does not prove every unit passes. Producer and consumer risks, critical characteristics, destructive testing, customer/regulatory requirements and process history affect plan choice.

Never reuse an old plan without confirming applicability and switching history.
