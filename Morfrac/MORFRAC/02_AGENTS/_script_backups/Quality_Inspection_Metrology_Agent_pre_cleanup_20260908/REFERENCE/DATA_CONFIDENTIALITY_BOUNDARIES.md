# Data, confidentiality and system boundaries

Quality master candidates may include characteristic mappings, inspection methods, equipment identifiers/status, calibration rules, decision rules, sampling plans, NCR/CAPA structures and templates. Preserve source, evidence, owner, effective state and history.

Do not copy prices, margins, discounts or supplier-commercial terms into Quality records. Do not expose customer complaints, failures, NCRs, calibration limitations, serial/lot or release status outside authorised need-to-know scope.

Credentials and signatures never belong in prompts, notes, evidence packs or master data.
