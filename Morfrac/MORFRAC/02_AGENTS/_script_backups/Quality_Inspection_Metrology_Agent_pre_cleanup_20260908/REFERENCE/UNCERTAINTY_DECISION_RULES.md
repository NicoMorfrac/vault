# Uncertainty and decision rules

An uncertainty evaluation identifies the measurand/model and relevant components, standardises them, accounts for sensitivities/correlations, combines them and states coverage. Use the approved GUM/JCGM or application-specific method and competent review.

Conformity near a specification limit depends on an agreed decision rule. Record guard bands, shared-risk treatment, uncertainty and rounding before looking for a preferred result. If evidence does not support conformity or nonconformity, use an indeterminate outcome.

A PASS label without the rule, limits and underlying result is not adequate evidence.
