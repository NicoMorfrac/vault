# Contact, Interactions and Connectors

Inventory every interface and justify bonded, contact, free, shrink fit, friction, no-penetration, thermal resistance or other interaction. Record initial gap/interference, friction/preload, allowable separation/slip and formulation.

Define bolt, pin, bearing, spring, elastic support, rigid/link and weld connectors with source properties and force extraction. Review global/component/local precedence.

Do not use contact stabilization, ignored gaps or automatic detection as unquestioned physics. Verify active contact regions, penetration, opening/slip and force balance.
