# Buckling, Modal and Dynamic Analysis

Eigenvalue buckling identifies idealized bifurcation factors/modes; review imperfections, preload, material/geometric nonlinearity, contact and post-buckling before treating capacity.

Modal studies require verified mass, stiffness, fixtures, connectors and prestress. Check total/effective modal mass, mode shapes and frequency convergence. Natural frequency alone is not forced response.

Dynamic response requires excitation, damping, mode coverage, time/frequency resolution, initial conditions and suitable output measures. Separate peak, RMS, PSD and fatigue use.
