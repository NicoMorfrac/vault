# Study Type and Nonlinearity

Select the study from the governing physics and licence capability. Review:

- geometric nonlinearity: large displacement/rotation, follower load, instability;
- material nonlinearity: plasticity, hyperelasticity, creep or nonlinear constitutive response;
- boundary/contact nonlinearity: changing contact, friction, separation, preload;
- transient/rate, inertia, damping, thermal or fatigue effects.

Linear static assumptions require proportional loading, small deformation, linear elastic material and boundary/contact behaviour compatible with linearization. Record excluded nonlinearities and consequence.
