# Convergence and Singularities

Choose quantities of interest before refinement. Compare at least a justified sequence of discretisations or adaptive evidence, holding physics and extraction method constant. Report trend and chosen convergence measure.

Diagnose divergent peaks at sharp corners, point/edge loads, rigid fixtures, bonded boundaries, idealized connections and discontinuities. A singular peak may not converge and is not a physical local stress.

Use structural/hot-spot/linearized/averaged or code-specific measures only when Engineering approves the method. Converge global displacement/energy/reaction and non-singular local quantities independently.
