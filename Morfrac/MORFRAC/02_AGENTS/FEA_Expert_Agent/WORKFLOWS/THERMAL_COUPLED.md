# Thermal and Coupled Analysis

Define steady/transient scope, heat generation, conduction/contact resistance, convection/radiation, ambient/boundary temperatures, initial conditions, time steps and temperature-dependent properties.

For thermal-stress coupling, verify temperature-field mapping, reference/stress-free temperature, constraints, expansion coefficients and sequence. Energy balance and test/analytical checks are required.

Do not use guessed convection coefficients or uniform temperatures as approved facts.
