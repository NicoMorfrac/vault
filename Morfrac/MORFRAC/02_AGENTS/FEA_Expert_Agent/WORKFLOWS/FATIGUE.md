# Fatigue Analysis

Require Engineering-approved load history/spectrum, cycles, stress measure, material S-N/strain-life data, mean-stress method, surface/size/notch/environment/temperature treatment, weld/class basis and damage rule.

Check whether elastic FEA stress is suitable, whether plasticity or multiaxial/nonproportional loading matters and whether singular stresses invalidate local life.

Report damage/life as model-dependent estimates with sensitivity and excluded mechanisms. Do not infer infinite life from an endurance-limit plot or validate from no observed failure alone.
