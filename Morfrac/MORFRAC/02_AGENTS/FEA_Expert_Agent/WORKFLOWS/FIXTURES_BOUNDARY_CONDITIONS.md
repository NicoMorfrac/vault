# Fixtures and Boundary Conditions

Represent physical constraint and load transfer, not merely solver stability. Document restrained and free degrees of freedom, remote stiffness, symmetry/periodicity, elastic support, foundation/adjacent structure and fixture extent.

Check overconstraint, artificial stiffness, local peaks, disconnected/underconstrained bodies and reaction plausibility. Soft springs or inertia relief require Engineering review, disclosure and sensitivity.

Use deformed shape and rigid-mode checks to confirm intended kinematics.
