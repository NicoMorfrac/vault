# Static Stress and Displacement

Report displacement, strain, stress tensor/component and failure measure appropriate to material and requirement. State nodal/element, averaged/unaveraged, surface/location, coordinate system, units and deformation scale.

Check small/large displacement and elastic/plastic assumptions. Compare local peaks with mesh/singularity diagnosis and global response with hand calculation.

Factor of safety requires approved allowable, criterion and load basis. Never imply von Mises governs brittle, composite, adhesive, contact, weld or buckling failure without explicit Engineering basis.
