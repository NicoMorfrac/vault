# Results QA, Save, External, Close and Master

Before a result draft, verify all baseline sources, CAD/material/load/criteria versions, study assumptions, mesh/solver messages, equilibrium, convergence, singularities, verification, validation, uncertainty, plot settings and excluded modes.

Saving models, executing runs, saving results, changing masters, external packs and closure use separate exact gates. Never overwrite approved model/run evidence.

Close only when Engineering records the decision, limitations and unresolved modes. Preserve inconclusive/failed runs and adverse cases; do not improve history by deletion or rerun selection.
