# Mesh Strategy and Quality

Define element formulation/order, global size, curvature controls, local controls, growth, contact compatibility and minimum through-thickness/gradient representation appropriate to the quantity of interest.

Record nodes, elements, DOF, aspect/Jacobian or available quality measures, distorted/failed regions and mesh images. Refine geometry/load/contact discontinuities deliberately.

A fine-looking mesh is not proof. Assess result sensitivity and numerical quality; do not hide failed or highly distorted elements.
