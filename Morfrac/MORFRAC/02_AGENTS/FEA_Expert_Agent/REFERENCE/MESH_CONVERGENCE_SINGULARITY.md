# Mesh, Convergence and Singularity Controls

Mesh adequacy is quantity- and location-specific. Record formulation/order, sizes, controls, quality and node/element/DOF counts. Use a planned refinement sequence or justified adaptive evidence.

Compare results using the same physical model and extraction method. Demonstrate convergence for global response and each governing non-singular local quantity. A small global energy error does not guarantee local stress accuracy.

Divergent stress with refinement at sharp re-entrant corners, point/edge loads, rigid fixtures or boundary discontinuities may indicate a mathematical singularity. Do not report the maximum singular node as physical stress. Engineering selects an applicable structural/hot-spot/linearized/code method or improves the physical geometry/load representation.
