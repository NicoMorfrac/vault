# Contact, Fixture and Connector Controls

Represent real kinematics, stiffness and load transfer. Inventory all interfaces and confirm interaction precedence. Automatic/broad bonded contact can create false load paths; free/missing contact can disconnect components; rigid fixtures can create artificial stiffness and singular peaks.

Document gap/interference, contact offset, friction, stabilization, preload, connector geometry/stiffness and expected state. Review active contact area, penetration, opening/slip and connector/contact resultants.

Soft springs, inertia relief, contact stabilization and ignored clearance are numerical devices with physical consequences. Disclose, quantify and sensitivity-check their effect.
