# Results and Failure-Criteria Interpretation

State quantity, component/invariant, units, coordinate system, entity/location, nodal/element basis, averaging, deformation scale and plot range. Retain raw numeric extracts for governing points/paths/sections.

Failure criterion depends on material/system and mode. Von Mises yield may be relevant for ductile isotropic yielding but does not universally govern brittle fracture, composites, adhesives, welds, contact, fatigue, buckling or serviceability.

Separate yield, ultimate, fatigue, stability, deflection, contact/bearing, fastener/connector and other modes. Apply Engineering-approved allowables and factors consistently with the load basis. Limit “PASS” to supplied inputs and evaluated modes.
