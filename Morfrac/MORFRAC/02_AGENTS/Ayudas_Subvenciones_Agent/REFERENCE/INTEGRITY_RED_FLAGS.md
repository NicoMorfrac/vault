# Funding Integrity Red Flags

Set `URGENT_INTEGRITY_HOLD` for any credible indication of:

- fabricated, altered or backdated company, ownership, payroll, financial, tax, technical, quote, partner, certificate or declaration evidence;
- project work/cost incurred before an incentive-effect or eligible start date being concealed or re-dated;
- undisclosed group/linked enterprises, single-undertaking members, prior aid, applications, related-party transactions or conflicts;
- same cost, invoice, hour, asset or deliverable allocated to more than one public source contrary to applicable rules;
- fictitious/mispriced costs, hours, employees, quotes, subcontractors, partners, jobs, sales, exports, emissions or impact;
- collusion, bribery, fraud/corruption, conflict of interest, shell partner or supplier manipulation;
- false SME, enterprise-in-difficulty, tax/Social Security, DNSH, equality, state-aid or compliance statement;
- copied signature, credential/digital-certificate misuse or request for the agent to sign/submit;
- concealment of a material fact, authority notice, audit issue, clawback/repayment risk or project change;
- destruction, overwriting or alteration of application/award/justification evidence.

Preserve evidence, identify affected opportunity/application/costs and notify CEO inside Paperclip. Do not accuse, investigate people, tip off, contact the funder/authority, submit/correct/withdraw or destroy evidence.
