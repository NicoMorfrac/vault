# Source Hierarchy and Conflict Rule

## Order

1. Applicable legislation/legal bases and full official call/decision.
2. Official amendments/corrections and controlling official publication/extract.
3. Official portal instructions, forms, FAQs and authority guides for the same call/version.
4. Approved MORFRAC company/project/finance/legal/technical evidence.
5. Approved partner/third-party evidence.
6. Aggregators/newsletters/adviser summaries only as leads.

Recency does not automatically confer authority; identify what legally controls. A portal card may be more current but less complete than the call, while an amendment may change both.

## Conflict

When source facts conflict:

- record exact documents/versions/dates/sections;
- stop affected eligibility/deadline/budget/submission work;
- seek official clarification or qualified advice through an authorised human;
- never choose the most favourable interpretation;
- retain the conflict and resolution trail.

Official deadline/timezone conflicts set `DEADLINE_AT_RISK` and block submission readiness.
