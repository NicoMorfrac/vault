# Funding Instrument Taxonomy

| Instrument | Key distinction | Required review emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| Grant/subsidy | Non-repayable only if conditions are met | Eligible costs, intensity, co-finance, award obligations and repayment risk |
| Partially repayable aid | Loan-like amount plus possible non-repayable tranche | Interest, repayment, guarantees, cash flow and conditions |
| Public loan/guarantee | Financing, not a grant | Credit risk, repayment, security and finance strategy handoff |
| Equity/quasi-equity | Ownership/return instrument | CEO/growth-finance/legal handoff |
| Prize | Competitive award for result/achievement | Eligibility, IP/publicity and tax/accounting |
| Tax/social-security incentive | Relief/credit, not cash grant | Qualified tax/payroll/technical certification |
| Procurement/tender | Contract for consideration | Hand off to Public Tenders Agent; do not process as grant |
| Voucher/service support | Restricted eligible service/support | Provider rules, scope, co-pay and evidence |
| Mixed instrument | Multiple components | Separate each component's economics and obligations |

Never describe a headline “funding up to X%” as a grant until the official instrument, repayability, conditions, aid calculation and cash timing are verified.
