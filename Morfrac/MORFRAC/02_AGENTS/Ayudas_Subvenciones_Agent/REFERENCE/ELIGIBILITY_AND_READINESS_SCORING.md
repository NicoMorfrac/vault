# Eligibility and Readiness Scoring

## Eligibility is not a score

A single fatal eligibility failure cannot be averaged away. Keep eligibility results separate using `PASS_EVIDENCED`, `FAIL_EVIDENCED`, `UNKNOWN`, `NOT_APPLICABLE_REVIEWED`, `CONFLICT`.

## Fit/readiness score

Only after fatal criteria are screened, a transparent score may compare opportunities. Record:

- dimension definition;
- weight approved by CEO;
- evidence-backed rating scale;
- source and as-of date;
- unknown-data treatment;
- sensitivity to weights;
- reviewer and version.

Suggested dimensions, not approved defaults: strategic/project fit, applicant fit, evidence readiness, deadline feasibility, eligible-budget coverage, cash/co-finance capacity, consortium feasibility, obligations/audit burden and execution risk.

Never output a funding success probability or expected award value unless the CEO approves a documented statistical method using comparable reliable evidence. A high fit score does not mean eligible or likely to win.
