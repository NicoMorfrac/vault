# Official Source Baseline

Baseline checked: 2026-08-30. This is a discovery starting point, not eligibility or availability advice. Calls, budgets, deadlines, amendments, FAQs, portals, thresholds and legal frameworks change. Verify the full official call and exact applicant/project at each decision.

## Spain

- Sistema Nacional de Publicidad de Subvenciones y Ayudas Públicas / BDNS: https://www.infosubvenciones.es/bdnstrans/es/index
- Boletín Oficial del Estado: https://www.boe.es/
- Ley 38/2003, General de Subvenciones: https://www.boe.es/eli/es/l/2003/11/17/38/con
- Real Decreto 887/2006, Reglamento de la Ley General de Subvenciones: https://www.boe.es/eli/es/rd/2006/07/21/887/con
- Plan de Recuperación, Transformación y Resiliencia and official calls: https://planderecuperacion.gob.es/
- Orden HFP/1030/2021, PRTR management including state aid, double funding, DNSH and integrity controls: https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2021-15860
- CDTI aid index: https://www.cdti.es/indice-de-ayudas
- CDTI grants: https://www.cdti.es/ayudas-subvenciones2
- CDTI R&D projects/partially repayable aid: https://www.cdti.es/ayudas/proyectos-de-i-d

Search the competent autonomous-community, provincial and local official bulletin/authority portals only after the applicant and project locations are verified. Do not assume one region from a project name or user location.

## European Union

- European Commission funding overview and programmes: https://commission.europa.eu/funding-and-tenders/find-funding_en
- EU calls by topic / Funding & Tenders routes: https://commission.europa.eu/funding-and-tenders/find-funding/find-calls-funding-topic_en
- EU funding and tender process overview: https://commission.europa.eu/live-work-travel-eu/funding-opportunities_en
- De minimis Regulation (EU) 2023/2831: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/2831/oj
- General Block Exemption Regulation (EU) No 651/2014: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2014/651/oj
- Commission Recommendation 2003/361/EC on SME definition: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reco/2003/361/oj

For each call use its named legal framework and current consolidated/amending acts. The agent must not assume that de minimis, GBER, SME status, Horizon or PRTR rules apply simply because public money is involved.
