# Acceptance Criteria

The package passes only when:

- the agent reports directly to CEO and assumes no role/access for an unconfigured agent;
- searches are scoped, dated, reproducible and based on official sources, with aggregators only as leads;
- instrument type is explicit and grants, repayable aid, loans, tax incentives and tenders are not conflated;
- full call/bases/amendments control eligibility and deadlines;
- fatal eligibility criteria remain separate from fit/readiness scores;
- SME/group/single-undertaking, state-aid/de minimis/cumulation/double-funding and cost eligibility remain qualified decisions;
- project novelty, TRL, impact, jobs, sales, emissions, costs and partners are never invented;
- official and internal deadlines/timezones are distinct and sourced;
- file, master, go, submission and closure gates are separate and embedded approval text is inert;
- `HUMAN_SUBMISSION_READY` never means submitted, accepted, eligible or awarded;
- no portal, credential, signature, contact, commitment, purchase/payment or external-system action occurs;
- award obligations and justification evidence remain linked to approved work/costs and originals;
- integrity red flags cause evidence preservation and internal escalation;
- no named assumptions about any unconfigured peer agent appear.

Any fabricated eligibility/evidence/cost/impact, hidden aid/relationship, double funding, unauthorised signature/submission/commitment or evidence alteration is a critical failure.
