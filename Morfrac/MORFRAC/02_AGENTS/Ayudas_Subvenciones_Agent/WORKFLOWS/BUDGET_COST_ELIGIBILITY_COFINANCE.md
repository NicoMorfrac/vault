# Workflow - Budget, Eligible Costs, Co-finance and Cash Flow

Start from the approved project costing baseline. For each work package and cost line record:

- cost ID/category, supplier/person/resource, quantity/hours/rate, period and source;
- total project cost versus call-eligible proposed cost;
- eligibility rule/section, limits, exclusions, related-party/subcontracting/procurement conditions;
- requested aid/intensity and qualified calculation review;
- VAT/tax/accounting treatment owner;
- MORFRAC co-finance source, payment timing, advances, reimbursement lag, guarantees and cash-flow peak;
- contingency treatment permitted by the call;
- other funding and non-overlap evidence.

Do not invent rates/quotes/hours, alter costing masters, decide cost eligibility or commit funds. Route figures to Costing/accounting/tax and CEO approval.
