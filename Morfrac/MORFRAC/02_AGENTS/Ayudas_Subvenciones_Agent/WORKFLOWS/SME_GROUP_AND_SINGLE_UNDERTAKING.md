# Workflow - SME, Group and Single Undertaking

Prepare a support matrix for qualified Legal/accounting review:

- applicant and all direct/indirect owners;
- autonomous, partner and linked enterprise relationships with voting/control evidence;
- consolidated/individual headcount, turnover and balance-sheet periods/sources;
- acquisitions, disposals and relationship changes affecting reference periods;
- group/control through persons or agreements;
- call-specific SME definition and effective date;
- single-undertaking perimeter for de minimis and relevant aid registers.

Do not decide SME or single-undertaking status from MORFRAC alone, a registry label, one year's accounts or employee count. Record the reviewer decision and exact applicability to each call.
