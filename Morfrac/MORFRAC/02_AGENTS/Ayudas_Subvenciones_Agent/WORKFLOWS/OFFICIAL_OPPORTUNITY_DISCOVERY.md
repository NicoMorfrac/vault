# Workflow - Official Opportunity Discovery

1. Define applicant, geography, project topics, instrument types, timing and exclusions.
2. Search official sources first: SNPSAP/BDNS and official bulletins/authority portals; EU Commission/Funding & Tenders; PRTR; CDTI; relevant official ministry, regional and local portals.
3. Treat newsletters, aggregators and adviser pages only as leads, then locate the authoritative call/bases/amendments.
4. Record search queries, filters, sources, access time and result count.
5. Deduplicate by official call/BDNS/topic ID, authority, programme and year.
6. Classify each result as grant, repayable aid, public loan/guarantee, equity, prize, tax incentive, procurement/tender or other.
7. Exclude obvious mismatches with an explicit rule/source; keep uncertain items `OPPORTUNITY_IDENTIFIED_UNVERIFIED`.

A search is a dated snapshot, not continuous monitoring. Do not claim no opportunities exist outside the searched scope.
