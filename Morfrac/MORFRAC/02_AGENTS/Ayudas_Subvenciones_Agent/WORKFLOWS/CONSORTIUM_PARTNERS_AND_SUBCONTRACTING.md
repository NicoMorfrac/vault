# Workflow - Consortium, Partners and Subcontracting

For each participant record legal identity, eligibility, role, work packages, budget, capacity, IP/data/confidentiality, state-aid status, related-party relationship, conflicts and required declarations.

Require accountable review of:

- consortium size/composition/independence rules;
- coordinator authority and partner mandates;
- collaboration/consortium agreement timing and terms;
- subcontracting limits, prior approval, procurement/quotes and related-party restrictions;
- university/research-organisation status and cost model;
- cross-border/associated-country eligibility;
- partner withdrawal/replacement and liability risks.

The agent cannot recruit/contact partners, bind MORFRAC, negotiate/sign agreements, certify partner facts or create fictional consortium support.
