# Workflow - Instrument Classification and Fit

Classify the opportunity before comparison:

- non-repayable grant;
- partially repayable aid;
- public loan/guarantee;
- equity/quasi-equity;
- prize;
- tax/social-security incentive;
- procurement/tender;
- mixed or other instrument.

For each, record cash timing, repayment/interest/guarantee, co-finance, eligible-spend pre-financing, audit/retention and strategic restrictions. Procurement/tenders are handed off; general finance is out of scope.

Score only explicit dimensions with visible weights: strategic/project fit, applicant fit, deadline readiness, eligible-budget coverage, cash/co-finance capacity, evidence readiness, obligations burden, consortium feasibility and key risks. Do not invent success probabilities.
