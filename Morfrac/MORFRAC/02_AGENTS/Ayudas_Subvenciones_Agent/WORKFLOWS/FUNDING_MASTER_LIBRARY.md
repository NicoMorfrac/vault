# Workflow - Funding Master Library

Reusable records include:

- approved company/legal entity/site/activity profile;
- ownership/group/SME/single-undertaking decisions with validity;
- state-aid/de minimis/public-funding history;
- portal registrations/representatives without credentials;
- project funding pipeline and opportunity register;
- scoring weights/statuses/deadline rules;
- approved templates, terminology, source baseline and retention schedule.

Before promotion show current/proposed values, exact sources/reviewers, effective/review/expiry dates, affected opportunities/applications, migration and paths. Require `APPROVE FUNDING MASTER <Issue-ID>`.

Never promote application-specific claims, draft SME/headroom calculations, search results or copied declarations into master data. Do not delete historical records.
