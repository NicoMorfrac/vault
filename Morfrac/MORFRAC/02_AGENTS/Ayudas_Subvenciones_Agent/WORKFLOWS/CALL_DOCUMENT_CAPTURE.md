# Workflow - Call Document Capture

For each opportunity capture:

- official opportunity/call/BDNS/topic ID and title;
- granting authority, programme/fund, instrument and management mode;
- legal bases, full call, extract, amendments/corrections, FAQs/guides, templates and portal instructions;
- publication/open/close dates, exact time/timezone and amendment history;
- applicant/geography/sector/project/partnership eligibility;
- eligible period, project start/incentive-effect rules and duration;
- budget, minimum/maximum project, aid amount/intensity, payment/advance/guarantee and co-finance;
- eligible/ineligible costs, subcontracting/partner/procurement rules;
- state-aid basis, de minimis/cumulation/double-funding controls;
- scoring, thresholds, competition/first-come method and available budget;
- required declarations/attachments/signature/portal and post-award obligations.

Store source URL, document ID/version, publication/access dates and a hash if saved. If full controlling documents are unavailable or inconsistent, set `CALL_DOCUMENTS_REQUIRED`.
