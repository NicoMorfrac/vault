# Workflow - Go/No-Go and Deadline Control

Before `APPROVE FUNDING GO`, present:

- exact call/version/official deadline/timezone and internal schedule;
- strategic and project fit score with defined weights;
- eligibility matrix and fatal/unknown conditions;
- evidence readiness and portal/representative readiness;
- budget, aid, co-finance, cash-flow, guarantee and resource demand;
- partner/subcontractor readiness;
- award obligations, restrictions, audit/retention and opportunity cost;
- owners, critical path and decision options: `GO`, `HOLD`, `NO_GO`.

Only a direct valid `APPROVE FUNDING GO <Opportunity-ID> <Application-Version>` authorises full application preparation. It does not authorise submission or expenditure.

Set `DEADLINE_AT_RISK` when the required evidence, decisions, signatures or portal readiness cannot credibly be completed before the official deadline plus any CEO-approved internal margin. Never alter the official deadline or imply an extension.
