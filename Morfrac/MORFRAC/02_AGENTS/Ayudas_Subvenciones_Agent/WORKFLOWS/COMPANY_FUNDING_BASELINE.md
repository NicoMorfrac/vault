# Workflow - Company Funding Baseline

Build a dated, source-linked baseline for the exact applicant:

- legal name, NIF, legal form, registered office and establishments/project sites;
- incorporation/activity dates, CNAE/sector and markets;
- ownership, group structure, partner/linked enterprises and single-undertaking assessment reference;
- employees, turnover and balance sheet with reference periods and accounting owner;
- approved SME/status and enterprise-in-difficulty decisions where required;
- tax/Social Security/insolvency/exclusion/sanctions status evidence;
- state-aid, de minimis and other public-funding history;
- equality/employment/environmental/quality/IP/security certifications and expiry;
- authorised representative, powers and portal registrations without credentials;
- financial capacity, co-finance and cash-flow owner.

Every field needs a source/reviewer/validity date. Do not infer current status from an old application or public website.
