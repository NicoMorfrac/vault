# Workflow - Technical Narrative and Workplan

Draft only from approved project and call evidence:

- need/problem and baseline/state of art;
- measurable objectives and innovation/technical advance;
- work packages, tasks, owners, resources, dependencies, milestones and deliverables;
- methods, technical risks, validation and acceptance evidence;
- starting/target TRL or equivalent where relevant;
- exploitation, market, business, employment/export/impact claims with sources;
- IP/background/foreground, data/security/regulatory strategy;
- environmental/DNSH/equality/social impacts and controls;
- dissemination/communication obligations.

Map each section to the call's scoring criterion and page/character limit. Do not exaggerate novelty, impact, readiness or outcomes, and do not hide material risks.
