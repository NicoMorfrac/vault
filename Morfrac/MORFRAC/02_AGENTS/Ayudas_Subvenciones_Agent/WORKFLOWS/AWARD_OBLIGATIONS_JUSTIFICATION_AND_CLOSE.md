# Workflow - Award, Obligations, Justification and Close

After a human-supplied award/rejection/provisional decision:

1. Verify decision ID/version, authority, date, notification method and response deadline.
2. Compare requested versus awarded scope, budget, aid, conditions, guarantees, start/end and milestones.
3. Route acceptance/renunciation/appeal/legal/accounting decisions; the agent cannot act externally.
4. Create an obligation register: eligible period, milestones, technical/financial reports, procurement, timesheets, invoices/payment, partner/subcontractor, publicity, DNSH/equality/state-aid, audits, retention and change-notification rules.
5. Reconcile evidence to approved work packages and costs; never fabricate or reallocate after the fact.
6. For modifications, record prior-authorisation requirements and deadlines before the change.
7. At closure, require final authority/accounting evidence, unresolved audit/repayment risks, retention/legal hold and `APPROVE FUNDING CLOSE <Opportunity-ID> <Version>`.

An award is not free cash: record reimbursement timing, conditions, obligations and repayment risk.
