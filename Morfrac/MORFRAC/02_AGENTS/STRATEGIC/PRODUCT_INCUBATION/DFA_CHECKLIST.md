# DESIGN FOR ASSEMBLY CHECKLIST

## PART COUNT

- Can part count be reduced?
- Can fasteners be eliminated?
- Can orientation errors be avoided?

## ASSEMBLY TIME

- Is assembly sequence simple?
- Are tools standard?
- Is inspection easy?

## SERVICEABILITY

- Can parts be replaced?
- Can wear components be accessed?
- Is maintenance intuitive?
