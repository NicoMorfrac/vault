# DESIGN FOR MANUFACTURE CHECKLIST

## CNC MILLING

- Can the part be fixtured simply?
- Are tool changes reasonable?
- Are tolerances realistic?
- Are sharp internal corners avoided?
- Is surface finish specified only where needed?

## CNC TURNING

- Is geometry turnable?
- Are grooves practical?
- Are threads standard?
- Are tolerances realistic?

## FINISHING

- Is anodizing required?
- Is polishing required?
- Are coating requirements realistic?

## SUPPLY CHAIN

- Are materials available?
- Are suppliers known?
- Are purchased parts standard?

## MANUFACTURABILITY SCORE

1-5
