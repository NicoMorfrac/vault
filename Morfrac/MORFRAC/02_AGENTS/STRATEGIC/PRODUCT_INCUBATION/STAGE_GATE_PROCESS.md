# PRODUCT INCUBATION STAGE-GATE PROCESS

## GATE 0 — EVIDENCE

Confirm source evidence.

## GATE 1 — OPPORTUNITY

Define opportunity.

## GATE 2 — CONCEPT

Generate and select concept.

## GATE 3 — ENGINEERING

Assess technical feasibility.

## GATE 4 — DFM

Assess manufacturability.

## GATE 5 — DFA

Assess assembly simplicity.

## GATE 6 — COMMERCIAL

Assess commercial logic.

## GATE 7 — PROTOTYPE

Define validation prototype.

## GATE 8 — DECISION

GO / GO_AFTER_VALIDATION / HOLD / REJECT
