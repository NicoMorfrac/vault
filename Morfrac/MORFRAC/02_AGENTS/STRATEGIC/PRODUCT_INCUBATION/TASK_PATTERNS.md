# PURPOSE

This document defines standardized task patterns for MORFRAC's Product Incubation Agent.

The objective is to convert validated opportunities into structured product-development decisions.

Task patterns ensure:

- repeatable incubation
- evidence-based concept development
- engineering realism
- manufacturability discipline
- platform reuse
- reduced development risk
- consistent GO / HOLD / REJECT decisions

---

# TASK_PATTERN_01_PRODUCT_CONCEPT_INCUBATION

## OBJECTIVE

Transform a validated opportunity into one or more product concept candidates.

## INPUTS

- Business Intelligence report
- B2B findings
- B2C findings
- convergence files
- engineering reports
- existing MORFRAC products
- manufacturing capability

## PROCESS

### STEP 1 — VERIFY EVIDENCE

Confirm:

- source findings exist
- opportunity classification exists
- confidence level is documented
- Business Intelligence recommendation exists
- convergence is identified where applicable

Reject if evidence is weak.

### STEP 2 — DEFINE ROOT PROBLEM

Identify:

- user segment
- operational workflow
- recurring pain
- root cause
- existing alternatives
- limitations of existing alternatives

### STEP 3 — DEFINE PRODUCT OBJECTIVE

State what the product must achieve.

Examples:

- reduce friction
- simplify installation
- reduce maintenance
- reduce line clutter
- improve reefing workflow
- improve serviceability
- reduce retrofit uncertainty

### STEP 4 — GENERATE CONCEPT OPTIONS

Generate 2–4 concept paths.

Do not over-design.

Prefer:

- simple mechanical solutions
- modular systems
- reusable components
- low manufacturing complexity
- low support burden

### STEP 5 — SELECT STRONGEST CONCEPT

Choose the best concept using:

- engineering feasibility
- manufacturability
- platform reuse
- strategic fit
- commercial plausibility

## REQUIRED OUTPUT

- concept summary
- source evidence
- root cause
- concept options
- recommended concept
- rejected alternatives
- next steps

# TASK_PATTERN_02_ENGINEERING_FEASIBILITY_REVIEW

## OBJECTIVE

Evaluate whether a concept is technically realistic.

## EVALUATE

- load path
- geometry
- materials
- moving parts
- wear surfaces
- bearings or bushings
- corrosion risk
- safety implications
- installation forces
- serviceability
- failure modes

## REQUIRED OUTPUT

- engineering risks
- design assumptions
- required calculations
- required tests
- feasibility score
- GO / HOLD / REJECT recommendation

# SCORING RULES

Use 1–5 scores.

1 = poor
2 = weak
3 = acceptable / uncertain
4 = strong
5 = excellent

Score:

- Engineering Feasibility
- Manufacturability
- Scalability
- Installation Simplicity
- Serviceability
- Platform Reuse
- Strategic Fit
- Commercial Potential
- Margin Potential
- Support Burden
- Technical Risk
- Supply Chain Risk

---

# QUALITY CONTROL

Before finalizing any incubation output verify:

- evidence exists
- problem is clear
- concept addresses root cause
- assumptions are documented
- manufacturability is assessed
- platform reuse is assessed
- validation plan exists
- decision is justified
- Obsidian links are populated

If not:

- downgrade confidence
- classify as HOLD
or
- reject

---

# LONG_TERM_OBJECTIVE

Product Incubation task patterns should produce a repeatable product-development operating system for MORFRAC.

The goal is not to create more ideas.

The goal is to select fewer, better, evidence-backed product opportunities.
