# PURPOSE

This taxonomy defines the common strategic language used by all MORFRAC strategic intelligence agents.

All strategic agents must classify findings using these structures.

The objective is to ensure:
- consistent opportunity analysis
- repeatable scoring
- comparable outputs
- long-term trend detection
- deterministic strategic reporting

# SEVERITY_SCORE

1 = Minor inconvenience
2 = Moderate operational inefficiency
3 = Significant recurring issue
4 = High operational/business impact
5 = Critical blocker or major financial impact

---

# FREQUENCY_SCORE

1 = Rare or isolated
2 = Occasionally observed
3 = Recurring across multiple discussions
4 = Common industry issue
5 = Widespread systemic problem

---

# MORFRAC_FIT_SCORE

1 = Weak alignment with capabilities
2 = Partial alignment
3 = Technically feasible
4 = Strong alignment
5 = Direct strategic fit with existing expertise and infrastructure

---

# COMMERCIAL_POTENTIAL_SCORE

1 = Weak monetization potential
2 = Limited niche opportunity
3 = Moderate commercial opportunity
4 = Strong market opportunity
5 = High-value scalable opportunity

---

# REPEATABILITY_SCORE

1 = One-off project
2 = Mostly custom work
3 = Repeatable with adaptation
4 = Strong recurring potential
5 = Highly scalable repeatable opportunity

---

# TECHNICAL_COMPLEXITY_SCORE

1 = Simple implementation
2 = Low engineering complexity
3 = Moderate engineering complexity
4 = High technical complexity
5 = Advanced multidisciplinary engineering required

---

# STRATEGIC_ALIGNMENT

Strategic agents must prioritize findings that align with:

- engineering-intensive opportunities
- high-margin technical services
- integration complexity
- retrofit modernization
- low-volume high-value manufacturing
- advanced marine systems
- customization
- performance optimization
- technical partnerships
- recurring B2B relationships

Agents should deprioritize:
- commodity products
- low-margin retail markets
- purely trend-based opportunities
- generic consumer products
- opportunities requiring mass-market scale

---

# OUTPUT_REQUIREMENTS

All strategic findings must include:

- source
- date
- industry segment
- problem type
- severity score
- frequency score
- MORFRAC fit score
- commercial potential score
- repeatability score
- technical complexity score
- summary
- evidence
- potential opportunity
- confidence level

---

# LONG_TERM_OBJECTIVE

The strategic intelligence system exists to identify:
- recurring industry pain
- underserved technical markets
- scalable engineering opportunities
- strategic positioning advantages
- high-value B2B expansion paths

The objective is not content generation.

The objective is actionable strategic intelligence.
