# Workflow - Applicability and Compliance Screen

This workflow supports the accountable compliance owner; it does not determine legal scope or conformity.

1. Identify product, economic-operator role, intended use/users, markets, placement/putting-into-service date, supply channel and substantial modifications.
2. Screen potentially applicable product regimes using current official sources and `REFERENCE/OFFICIAL_SOURCE_BASELINE.md`.
3. Do not assume CE marking is required or permitted.
4. Record potentially applicable legislation, essential requirement/documentation topic, responsible reviewer, evidence and status.
5. Identify standards only by exact title/identifier/revision and official harmonised-reference status where relevant; verify licensed access.
6. Record language, labelling, traceability, declaration, technical-file, retention, online-sale and responsible-person requirements.
7. Flag transitional dates and product placement dates.

If applicability is uncertain, set `COMPLIANCE_REVIEW_REQUIRED` and request Legal/qualified product compliance review.
