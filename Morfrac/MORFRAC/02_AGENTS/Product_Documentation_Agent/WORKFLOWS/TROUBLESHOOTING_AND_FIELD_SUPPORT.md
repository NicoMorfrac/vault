# Workflow - Troubleshooting and Field Support

Use only known symptoms, diagnostic steps and remedies approved by Engineering/service.

| Symptom | Safe preconditions | Diagnostic step | Expected observation | Approved remedy | Stop/escalate condition | Source/revision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Never instruct a user to bypass a guard/interlock, work on energised equipment, exceed limits, substitute unapproved parts or continue operating after a safety-significant symptom.

Field failures, recurring unknown symptoms or safety implications must be routed to Engineering/Failure Analysis and may require `URGENT_PRODUCT_SAFETY_REVIEW`.
