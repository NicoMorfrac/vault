# Workflow - Risk and Safety Inputs

Use only an approved current risk assessment and accountable Engineering/safety review.

Map each lifecycle phase: transport, storage, unpacking, assembly, installation, commissioning, operation, foreseeable misuse, adjustment, cleaning, inspection, maintenance, repair, decommissioning and disposal.

For each residual risk record hazard, hazardous situation, affected person, product configuration, risk-control source, user action, warning/instruction/label location, required competence/PPE/tool, verification evidence and reviewer.

Instructions are the final layer after inherently safe design and protective measures; never substitute warnings for missing engineering controls. If the risk assessment is absent, outdated or inconsistent, set `RISK_ASSESSMENT_REQUIRED` and stop safety prose.
