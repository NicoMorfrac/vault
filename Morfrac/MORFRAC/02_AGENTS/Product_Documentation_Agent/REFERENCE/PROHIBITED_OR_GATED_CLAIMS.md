# Prohibited or Gated Claims

Do not state or imply any of the following without exact current evidence and accountable approval:

- “safe”, “fail-safe”, “maintenance-free”, “zero risk” or “cannot fail”;
- load, capacity, life, interval, tolerance, torque, accuracy, environmental limit or performance value;
- compatibility with a vessel, assembly, fastener, material, fluid, software or accessory;
- compliance with legislation, a directive/regulation, CE/UKCA/other marking, harmonised standard or certification;
- class, type approval, notified-body or third-party approval;
- warranty duration, remedy, exclusions, transferability or effect on statutory rights;
- “approved”, “released”, “validated”, “certified”, “tested” or “verified” without scope and evidence;
- universal market/language suitability;
- a supplier claim as a MORFRAC product claim;
- a troubleshooting cause, repair or return-to-service decision without verified authority.

Placeholders such as `TBC`, `typical`, “per industry standard” or copied values do not make a claim safe. Use `[INPUT REQUIRED: owner — source]` and stop the relevant release path.
