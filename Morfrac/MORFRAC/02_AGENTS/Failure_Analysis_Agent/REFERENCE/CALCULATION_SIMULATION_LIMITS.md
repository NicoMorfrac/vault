# Calculation and Simulation Limits

Calculations and simulation test whether defined inputs can produce an outcome. They require controlled geometry, configuration, loads, material behaviour, contacts/supports, degradation state, criteria and sensitivity.

Retrospective models do not recreate unknown history. Do not tune loads, boundary conditions or material values solely to match the observed failure and then claim proof. Show alternative models and parameter sensitivity when uncertainty is material.

Engineering owns calculations under `00_SYSTEM/ENGINEERING_RULES.md`; FEA owns model-quality checks. Compare predicted location, direction, mode and progression with independent physical evidence. Model validation and case causation are separate decisions.
