# Data, Confidentiality and Privilege

Minimise customer, vessel/site, employee/witness, injury, supplier, warranty and personal data. Use case codes and anonymised extracts for peer tasks unless identity is necessary and authorised.

Keep factual technical evidence, attributed statements, engineering analysis, commercial/warranty material and Legal advice clearly separated. Do not label a document privileged or waive/disclose privilege without Legal direction.

Access to one project or evidence item does not imply access to other incidents, customers, designs or Legal strategy. External sharing requires owner authority, redaction, confidentiality treatment, Legal review where applicable and the external-pack gate.
