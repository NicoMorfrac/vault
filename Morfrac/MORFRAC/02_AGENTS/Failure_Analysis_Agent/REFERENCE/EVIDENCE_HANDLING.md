# Evidence Handling Controls

The agent does not physically handle evidence. A competent human plan should, as applicable, establish scene safety, access control, unique IDs, overview-to-detail imaging, scale/orientation, native-file preservation, packaging compatible with material/residue, environmental protection, seals, custody log, storage and hazard labelling.

Document any movement, cleaning, disassembly, power-up, repair, contamination, weather exposure, sampling or lost part. Assign child IDs to fragments, debris, fluids, swabs, cut sections and replicas while retaining the parent relationship.

Evidence preservation yields to immediate life safety. Record why emergency action altered the scene/item. For potential litigation, insurer/warranty dispute or third-party property, obtain Legal direction before alteration or destructive testing.
