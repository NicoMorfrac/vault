# Human and Organisational Factors — No-Blame Control

Investigate conditions that shaped action: task/procedure design, tools/access, information, interface/alarms, workload/time pressure, competence, supervision, maintenance, change control, staffing, communication and management systems.

Record facts and attributed statements. Do not infer intent, diagnose a person, conduct covert investigation, rank witness honesty or turn a deviation into root cause without asking why controls allowed it.

Keep technical learning separate from disciplinary, employment, criminal, insurer and liability decisions. Those decisions belong to authorised humans and Legal/PRL advisers.
