# Official Source Baseline

Accessed 2026-08-30. Re-open the official page, verify current status/revision and retain the licensed/authorised source used for every live case. Titles and scope summaries below do not reproduce the standards and do not establish applicability.

## Technical-investigation evidence

- ASTM E1188-23, collection and preservation by a technical investigator: <https://store.astm.org/e1188-23.html>
- ASTM E1459-24, physical-evidence labelling and documentation: <https://store.astm.org/e1459-24.html>
- ASTM E1492-25, receiving, documenting, storing and retrieving evidence: <https://store.astm.org/e1492-25.html>
- ASTM E860-22, examining/preparing items that may be involved in litigation: <https://store.astm.org/e0860-22.html>

These are licensed standards. Use the exact authorised text and competent professional judgment where applicable. They do not make the agent a forensic practitioner, custodian, laboratory or expert witness.

ASTM E2332-04, investigation and analysis of physical component failures, is withdrawn. It may be a historical lead only and must not be presented as a current standard: <https://store.astm.org/e2332-04.html>

## Machinery diagnostics and component-specific sources

- ISO 13379-1:2025, general guidelines for condition-monitoring data interpretation and diagnostics: <https://www.iso.org/standard/88027.html>
- ISO 15243:2017, rolling-bearing damage/failure terminology, characteristics and possible causes: <https://committee.iso.org/standard/59619.html?browse=ics>

On access date ISO showed ISO 15243:2017 as published but due to be revised. Bearing appearance is a comparison input, not case proof; additional investigation is required when visual characterization is insufficient.

## Incident and causal investigation

- INSST 2025 accident-investigation overview and cause-tree method: <https://www.insst.es/documents/d/portal-insst/tema-30-investigacion-de-accidentes-de-trabajo>
- INSST NTP 1046, collecting witness accounts: <https://www.insst.es/documentacion/colecciones-tecnicas/ntp-notas-tecnicas-de-prevencion/30-serie-ntp-numeros-1031-a-1065-ano-2015/ntp-1.046-investigacion-de-accidentes-recogida-de-testimonios>
- OSHA incident investigation: <https://www.osha.gov/incident-investigation>
- HSE investigating accidents and incidents: <https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/books/hsg245.htm>

These sources support fact collection, multiple/root causes, system contributors and prevention rather than blame. Spanish legal/occupational obligations require competent human and Legal/PRL review.

## Product-safety escalation

- Current consolidated Regulation (EU) 2023/988 on general product safety: <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj>
- European Commission Safety Business Gateway information: <https://webgate.ec.europa.eu/safety-business-gateway/>

Applicability, economic-operator duties, accident notification, recall/corrective action and authority contact are Legal/product-safety decisions. The agent identifies a review need and does not notify.

## Source capture

Record issuer, title, identifier/revision, publication/effective/status date, URL, access timestamp, licensed copy location, applicability decision owner and excerpts/requirements used. Vendor guides, images and generic web examples are discovery/comparison aids only.
