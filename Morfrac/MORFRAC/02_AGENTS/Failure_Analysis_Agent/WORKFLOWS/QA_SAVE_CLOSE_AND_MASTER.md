# QA, Save, Close and Master Control

Before any controlled output, verify case/configuration, safety state, evidence register/custody, source links, facts-versus-opinions, hypothesis completeness, test approvals, calibration/competence, calculation/FEA limits, cause confidence, corrective traceability, Legal/Quality/Engineering reviews and unresolved risks.

Do not overwrite prior records. Saving, corrective plan, external pack, master change and closure each require their own exact gate.

Close only after the affected-population decision, corrective/verification status and evidence retention/legal-hold disposition are explicit. Preserve inconclusive cases as `ROOT_CAUSE_NOT_ESTABLISHED` rather than manufacturing certainty.
