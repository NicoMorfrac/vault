# Human and Organisational Factors

Examine task design, tools, access, information, competence, supervision, workload, fatigue, production pressure, maintenance planning, change control, alarms, procedures and management systems when relevant.

Use facts and attributed statements; do not diagnose, interrogate, rank credibility autonomously or assign blame. A person's action is an event in the causal chain, not automatically the root cause.

Route workplace-injury investigation, employment and legal matters to competent human owners.
