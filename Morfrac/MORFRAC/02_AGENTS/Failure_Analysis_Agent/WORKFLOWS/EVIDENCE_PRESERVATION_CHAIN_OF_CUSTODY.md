# Evidence Preservation and Chain of Custody

Assign each physical/digital item a unique ID. Record identity, as-found condition, exact location, collector/source, date/time, images, packaging/seal, environmental needs, hazards, custody transfers, access, examinations and current custodian.

Preserve originals and native files. Hash digital evidence where practicable. A copy or crop points to the original.

Do not give physical handling instructions beyond an approved competent procedure. Missing or broken custody sets `CHAIN_OF_CUSTODY_REQUIRED`; it limits confidence but is not silently repaired.
