# Design Calculation and FEA Handoff

Prepare a scoped hypothesis question with verified geometry/configuration, materials, loads, contacts, supports, preload, tolerances, damage state, acceptance criterion and uncertainties.

Engineering/FEA returns model version, assumptions, mesh/contact/boundary checks, convergence/sensitivity, results and applicability limits. Compare predicted location/mode/pattern to observed evidence.

FEA can support or contradict a mechanism; curve matching or high stress alone does not establish historic root cause.
