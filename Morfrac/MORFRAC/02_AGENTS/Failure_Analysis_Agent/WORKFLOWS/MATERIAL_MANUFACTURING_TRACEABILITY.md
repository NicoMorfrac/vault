# Material and Manufacturing Traceability

Trace specified versus received material, heat/lot, certificates, chemistry, mechanical properties, condition/temper, heat treatment, coating, forging/casting/weld, machining, surface finish, residual stress, inspection, nonconformance, rework and supplier chain.

Use approved vault material records for calculations. Certificates are evidence inputs and must match item/lot and test basis.

Manufacturing deviations require Quality/CNC/supplier evidence; do not infer a process defect from appearance alone.
