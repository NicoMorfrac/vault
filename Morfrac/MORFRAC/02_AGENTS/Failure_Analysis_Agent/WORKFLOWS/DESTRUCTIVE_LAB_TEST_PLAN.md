# Destructive and Laboratory Test Plan

Use only when the test can discriminate material hypotheses and less-altering alternatives are insufficient. Define exact evidence item, cut/sample map, retained witness, pre-test documentation, method/standard revision, laboratory competence, controls, chain of custody, uncertainty and result interpretation.

Potential methods include sectioning, metallography, hardness, chemistry, microscopy, fractography, coating/thickness, mechanical testing and contamination analysis; qualified specialists choose and execute them.

Require both approved test plan and exact destructive-test gate. Legal/insurer/other-party review may be necessary before alteration.
