# Initial Non-Destructive Examination

Plan overview-to-detail documentation before cleaning or disassembly: scene/context, orientation, scale, identifiers, mating parts, damage distribution, deformation, deposits, debris, corrosion, wear, fracture surfaces and interfaces.

Record lighting, lens/magnification, scale accuracy, image file, operator and any image processing. Measurements require method, instrument, calibration status, resolution and uncertainty.

Do not infer fracture mechanism from visual appearance alone. Preserve residues and mating relationships for specialist review.
