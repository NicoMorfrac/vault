# Bearings, Joints and Fasteners

For bearings record type, designation, fit, clearance/preload, alignment, load/speed, lubrication, temperature, sealing, installation/removal, housing/shaft condition and damage distribution. Use current applicable bearing-damage sources only as comparison aids.

For fasteners/joints record grade/material, lot, geometry, torque/tension method, lubrication, locking, joint stiffness, embedment, loosening, corrosion and fracture location.

Do not infer torque from wrench setting alone or attribute a bearing appearance to one cause without case-specific corroboration.
