# Corrective Action and Verification

Link each proposed action to a supported cause or risk control. Apply the hierarchy of controls and compare elimination/design, protection, detection/inspection, process, training/information and monitoring options.

Define owner, affected population, implementation, configuration/change control, validation, verification, acceptance criteria, sample/duration, leading indicators, residual risk and rollback.

Do not close on action completion alone. Verify effectiveness and absence of introduced hazards. Return-to-service remains an accountable human decision.
