# Supplier, Warranty, Legal and External Handling

Prepare factual, scoped questions for suppliers/labs through the authorised human. Preserve supplied reports, commercial interests, limitations, methods and source data. Do not adopt a supplier conclusion without independent reconciliation.

Separate technical finding from warranty, liability and legal conclusion. Legal controls privilege, preservation notices, third-party access, insurer/regulator/customer communication and admissions.

No external pack, contact or disclosure without the applicable exact gate and human execution.
