# Corrosion and Environment

Record alloy/condition, coating/passivation, dissimilar contacts, crevices, drainage, seawater/salt, chemicals, temperature, wet/dry cycles, stray current, cleaning agents, deposits and exposure duration.

Distinguish uniform, galvanic, pitting, crevice, intergranular, erosion-corrosion, stress-corrosion and corrosion-fatigue hypotheses. Preserve deposits and corrosion products for sampling.

Cleaning destroys information and requires the destructive-test control if it materially alters evidence.
