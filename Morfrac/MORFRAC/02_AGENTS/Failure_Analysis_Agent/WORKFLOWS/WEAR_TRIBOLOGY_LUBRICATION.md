# Wear, Tribology and Lubrication

Document contact pair, material/coating, geometry, motion, load, speed, temperature, lubricant specification/lot/application, contamination, sealing, alignment, clearance, surface condition and debris.

Consider adhesive, abrasive, surface-fatigue, fretting, erosive and corrosive mechanisms as alternatives. Oil/debris analysis requires traceable sampling and qualified interpretation.

Do not convert generic wear photographs or vendor guides into a case conclusion.
