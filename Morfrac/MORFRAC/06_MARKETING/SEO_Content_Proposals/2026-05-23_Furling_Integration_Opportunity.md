## Authority Opportunity Summary

The highest-value authority opportunity is not a generic “what is furling” article. It is a system-level authority piece focused on why furling integrations fail or underperform when the furling unit, textile interface, line path, deck geometry, and load path are treated as separate decisions instead of one engineered system.

This topic matters because the real pain does not usually come from the furling drum alone. It appears at the interfaces:

- continuous-line routing that adds drag or inconsistent handling
- top-down integration that loses control under load
- textile terminations and interface hardware that do not match the working geometry
- retrofit installs where deck layout, tack position, or lead angles constrain the whole system
- attempts to simplify hardware that accidentally move friction or side-load elsewhere

The audience is specifiers, riggers, sailmakers, builders, and performance owners dealing with furling retrofits, constrained foredeck layouts, or high-load handling systems.

MORFRAC is positioned to speak credibly here because the company already sits across the relevant entities and interfaces rather than only one SKU type. The current commercial footprint includes `powerfurl`, `morfring`, `mloop`, dogbone-related connectors, shackles, and custom/underdeck furling variants. That gives MORFRAC a defensible right to explain integration logic, not just component features.
