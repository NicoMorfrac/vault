Executive Commentary

MORFRAC has a short-term traffic bounce but a still-deteriorating 28-day base. The business issue is not raw volume; it is quality, relevance, and search visibility.

  • GA4 7-day sessions rose to 224 (+20.4%), but 28-day sessions remain down sharply at 774 (-33.9%).
  • Organic performance weakened materially: clicks -37.5%, CTR -34.8%, and average position worsened from 11.46 to 15.41.
  • Traffic quality is mixed-to-weak. Nearly half of analyzed traffic sits in low-tier / low-confidence geographies, while direct traffic dominates but is not commercially informative on its own.
  • The strongest commercial signal is Spain, followed by the US, Germany, and Italy, but much of the traffic mix is not yet high-confidence demand.
  • The best leverage is not “more traffic”; it is fixing SEO CTR/rank erosion and building technically credible content around dogbones, padeyes, soft pad eyes, and related load-path hardware.
  • Competitor signals show no detected changes, so the current issue is more likely internal visibility/positioning loss than an obvious competitor move.
