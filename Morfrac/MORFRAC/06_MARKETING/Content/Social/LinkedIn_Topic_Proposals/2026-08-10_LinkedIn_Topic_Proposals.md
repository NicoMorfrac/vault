# Proposal 1

1. **Topic Title**  
   **Dogbones in High-Load Sailing Systems: Small Component, Critical Load Path**

2. **Why This Matters**  
   Dogbones often look simple, but in performance sailing they can determine load transfer, adjustment precision, and system reliability. The engineering choices behind geometry, material, and wear behavior directly affect efficiency and safety.

3. **Strategic Angle**  
   Position dogbones as a load-bearing interface rather than a commodity connector. Focus on how optimized design can improve articulation, reduce creep and chafe, and maintain predictable performance under cyclic high-load conditions.

4. **Discussion Potential**  
   Invite debate on:
   - Dyneema vs. other constructions for load-bearing links  
   - Wear life under repeated articulation  
   - How length and diameter affect system geometry and tuning  
   - When a dogbone becomes the limiting factor in an otherwise high-performance setup

5. **Recommended Tone**  
   Technical, precise, and engineering-led. Confident but practical, with emphasis on real-world load behavior and design trade-offs.
