1. **Topic Title**  
   **Dogbones in High-Load Sailing Systems: Small Component, Large Performance Impact**

2. **Why This Matters**  
   Dogbones often determine how efficiently load is transferred in a system. In performance sailing, their geometry, material choice, and wear behavior can affect friction, articulation, and reliability under dynamic load.
