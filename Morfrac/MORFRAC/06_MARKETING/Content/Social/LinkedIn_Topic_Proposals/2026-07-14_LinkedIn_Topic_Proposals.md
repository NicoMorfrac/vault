1. **Topic Title**  
   **Dogbone Terminations: Small Component, Large Impact on Load Path Efficiency**

2. **Why This Matters**  
   In performance sailing, dogbones influence how loads are transferred, how much friction is introduced, and how reliably the system behaves under cyclic loading. A poorly specified termination can compromise efficiency and durability.
