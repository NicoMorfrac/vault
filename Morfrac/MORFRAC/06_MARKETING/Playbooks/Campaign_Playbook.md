# MORFRAC Campaign Playbook

## Objective

Define how to convert insights into marketing campaigns.

---

## CRO CAMPAIGN

Trigger:
- High traffic + low conversion

Actions:

- Improve CTA
- Simplify page
- Add proof (projects, images)
- Reduce friction

Expected impact:
+20% to +100% conversions

---

## SEO SCALING CAMPAIGN

Trigger:
- Page performing well

Actions:

- Expand content
- Add related keywords
- Build backlinks
- Promote content

---

## TRAFFIC RECOVERY CAMPAIGN

Trigger:
- Traffic drop >20%

Actions:

- Identify cause
- Update content
- Fix SEO issues
- Re-promote page

---

## NEW OPPORTUNITY CAMPAIGN

Trigger:
- High impressions + low CTR
- Untapped queries

Actions:

- Create new page
- Optimize title
- Target niche

---

## COMPETITOR RESPONSE CAMPAIGN

Trigger:
- Competitor launching campaign
- Competitor outranking

Actions:

- Analyze their messaging
- Improve your page
- Counter-position
- Launch targeted content

---

## CONTENT CAMPAIGN

Trigger:
- Need to build authority

Actions:

- Write technical content
- Show expertise
- Answer real problems

---

## CAMPAIGN PRIORITY

High:
- revenue impact
- conversion issues
- major traffic drop

Medium:
- ranking improvements
- CTR optimization

Low:
- minor tweaks

---

## FINAL RULE

No campaign without:

- clear trigger
- clear metric
- clear expected outcome

If unclear:
→ do not execute
